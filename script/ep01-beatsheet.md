## SEQ 01 — THE BEDROOM (0:00 – 1:35)

**Function:** establish that Kit is genuinely good, then that he never finishes. Start the
print. Cross the threshold.

| # | Beat |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Cold open, macro.** A nozzle lays one glowing line. Stepper-motor music. Pull back to reveal the bedroom. |
| 2 | **The plant.** The blank white figurine in the pen cup, in frame, unremarked. |
| 3 | **He's good.** Kit modelling at speed — fast, loose, absorbed, genuinely skilled. No dialogue for eight seconds. Let the audience bank it. |
| 4 | **He's selling.** On a call with a friend, mid-pitch about the robot dog. Enormous claims. |
| 5 | **The friend's line.** "You said that about the last one." |
| 6 | **The drawer.** He opens it to find a part. Forty abandoned robot dogs. He closes it fast. One shot, no dialogue. |
| 7 | **The quiet beat.** Alone. Cursor over PRINT. He is visibly scared. **This is the shot that makes him likeable. Do not cut it.** |
| 8 | **The call.** He presses print. "Six hours. Tomorrow I bring a robot dog to school." |
| 9 | **Threshold.** He leans in to watch the first layer. The chamber light pulses. He dissolves feet-first, layer by layer, into the machine. |

**Out on:** an empty chair, the printer running, the figurine in the cup.

## SEQ 02 — THE RAFT: ARRIVAL (1:35 – 3:25)

**Function:** wonder. This is the Spirited Away sequence and it is nearly plotless on
purpose. Sixty-five of these one hundred and ten seconds are a boy looking at a city.

| # | Beat |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Under the river.** Warm amber, slow, muffled. He surfaces gasping. |
| 2 | **Benchy fishes him out.** "Don't drink the river." Kit drinks the river. |
| 3 | **THE REVEAL.** Crane up off the dock to the full width of the Raft. Held long. No dialogue. |
| 4 | **The sky.** He looks up. The underside of a desk, impossibly far away. He understands where he is. |
| 5 | **The Hand.** On the horizon, building. Everybody watches it the way you watch weather. |
| 6 | **Delight, not fear.** Kit's reaction is pure joy. He is the only character in film history to fall into another dimension and immediately start networking. |
| 7 | **The worst sentence.** "I made all of you." Total horrified silence. A stall-holder starts crying. |
| 8 | **The rules.** Benchy, four words at a time. You came in on a print. You leave when it finishes. |
| 9 | **The clock.** Benchy points at the Hand. "That's yours. Six hours. Don't be late." |
| 10 | **The market.** Pure wonder montage. Five unexplained things at the edge of frame. |

**Out on:** Kit, small, in an enormous street, grinning like an idiot.

## SEQ 03 — THE RAFT: THE CROWNING (3:25 – 4:50)

**Function:** the marker, the crown, the refusal-by-acceptance, and the Hand stops.

| # | Beat |
|---|---|
| 1 | **The Tower arrives.** Ten layers, one argument. Establishes the comic engine and delivers the lore in four lines nobody notices is exposition. |
| 2 | **The marker works.** Kit doodles absently and it *builds*. Halftone bloom, amber trail. He stops dead. |
| 3 | **The crowd goes up.** The Brim have seen this exactly once before. Prophecy. They crown him **The Maker**. |
| 4 | **He takes the crown.** He does not correct them. He escalates. This is the Refusal — he accepts a title instead of a job. |
| 5 | **The peak of the sell.** Kit's biggest speech in the film. Charming, funny, and entirely writing cheques he can't cash. |
| 6 | **JUNE.** Cuts through the crowd. Half of her is missing. She looks at him for about a second. |
| 7 | **"You're not going to finish it."** Public. Flat. Devastating. |
| 8 | **He doesn't argue.** He goes quiet. Everyone in the audience recognises that silence. |
| 9 | **THE HAND STOPS.** Mid-line. The sound drops out entirely. Every single person in the plaza looks up at once. |
| 10 | **Benchy:** "That's never happened." |

**Out on:** Kit realising the frozen thing on the horizon is his.

## SEQ 04 — DINO BASIN (4:50 – 6:15)

**Function:** the crossing, the shame of Colossus, and the first horror beat.

| # | Beat |
|---|---|
| 1 | **The team, badly assembled.** June guides them because she wants the Hand restarted for her own reasons, not for his. The Tower comes along uninvited. |
| 2 | **COLOSSUS catches up.** Dragging himself on two front legs, delighted. Boot-up chime. |
| 3 | **"That's not mine."** Kit's first lie. June hears it. |
| 4 | **The theme line.** Benchy, on the river, not looking at him. Four million boats. Somebody pressed the button. Kit does not acknowledge it and we do not cut to his face. |
| 5 | **The herd.** Spectacle. The most purely fun ninety seconds in the film. Low camera, enormous legs, dust. |
| 6 | **The jump.** Colossus can't make it. Kit almost turns back. Doesn't. June sees. |
| 7 | **The rise.** They crest a ridge. |
| 8 | **THE FINISHED VILLAGE.** Dozens of blank white motionless figures. Silence — cut the ambient bed entirely. Nobody speaks for a full ten seconds. |
| 9 | **The clock starts.** The white horizon, visible for the first time. From here it never retreats. |

**Out on:** the group very small, walking away from the village, the white line behind them.

## SEQ 05 — GEARHAVEN (6:15 – 7:25)

**Function:** the midpoint. The charm stops working. The mystery half-turns. The villain
introduces himself.

| # | Beat |
|---|---|
| 1 | **The bureaucracy.** Kit tries to talk his way in. It does not work at all. First real crack in him. |
| 2 | **The Tower vs the Union.** Ten layers arguing with a bureaucracy. Best comedy set piece in the film. Give it room. |
| 3 | **The Archive.** Enormous, dusty, silent. The record of everything ever made. |
| 4 | **The Hand was cut.** It didn't break. Somebody stopped it. |
| 5 | **It happened once before.** At the very beginning. On the first print. Which was a print of a person. |
| 6 | **The empty slot.** Slot one. Dust, and a clean outline where something stood for a very long time. |
| 7 | **June's stake.** The thing she's been waiting years for was murdered. She says almost nothing. |
| 8 | **THE FINISHER.** He is simply there. Warm, delighted, unhurried. He does not attack. |
| 9 | **He knows about the drawer.** "You've got a drawer full of it." Kit has told nobody. |
| 10 | **He leaves.** Politely. That's the whole scene. |

**Out on:** Kit alone in the frame, and the Finisher's total lack of a shadow.

## SEQ 06 — THE DEEP BIN (7:25 – 8:35)

**Function:** the Ordeal. Every beat hurts.

| # | Beat |
|---|---|
| 1 | **The descent.** Down into the dark. Colour drains out of the film. |
| 2 | **The Bin-Lost.** Broken, half-built, gentle. They come and look, and go back to waiting. Two are clearly somebody's hand-made originals. One is looking at June. |
| 3 | **This is where she's from.** She's been climbing out of here for years. |
| 4 | **The maker's mark.** `JUNIPER`. Four letters built. She has never told anyone. |
| 5 | **THE FINISHER TAKES COLOSSUS.** Gently. As a mercy. "Sweetheart. Look at you. Somebody stopped." |
| 6 | **Kit freezes.** He starts the line. Stops. Erases. Starts again. The moment passes while he is still deciding. **He fails by doing the exact thing he always does.** |
| 7 | **Colossus is smoothed.** Frozen mid-stride, one paw up. Total silence. |
| 8 | **The Tower loses four layers.** The survivors go quiet for the first time in the film. Layer 3 finally has the silence he always wanted. |
| 9 | **The admission.** Kit, out loud, to June: he has never let anyone have anything he made. |
| 10 | **She does not comfort him.** She gives him a deadline. |

**Out on:** Kit picking up the marker.

## SEQ 07 — THE SMOOTH (8:35 – 9:35)

**Function:** the climax. Sixty seconds, and it must not be a fight.

| # | Beat |
|---|---|
| 1 | **The white.** No rim light, no shadow, no separation. Physically uncomfortable after six sequences of hard graphic silhouettes. |
| 2 | **The Finisher explains.** He was first. He came out wrong. He was thrown away. The archive backed him up ninety seconds ago, so we know he isn't lying. |
| 3 | **THE OFFER.** He doesn't threaten. He offers Kit exactly what Kit already wants: never finish anything, and nothing will ever be wrong. |
| 4 | **Kit hesitates.** Visibly. The audience must see it. |
| 5 | **"Okay. It's gonna be bad."** He draws. Fast, ugly, no erasing, six seconds. |
| 6 | **Colossus is finished.** Mismatched, lopsided, whole. **He completes a bark for the first time in the film.** |
| 7 | **The crowd draws.** The Brim, at the edge, start scribbling. Badly. Hundreds of them. Hundreds of lopsided things arriving at once. |
| 8 | **He cannot smooth what is still being made.** Not a fight — an overwhelming. Colour floods back in a wave. Grade overshoots past peak for four seconds. |
| 9 | **The Hand restarts.** The film's signature sound, absent since SH-043, comes back. |
| 10 | **June.** She says no. He does it anyway, in four seconds, while she is mid-sentence. |
| 11 | **"It's crooked." / "Yeah." / "...Okay."** |
| 12 | **He's yanked out.** Mid-word. No goodbye. The print is done. |

**Out on:** June, complete and mismatched, alone in a world that has its colour back.
