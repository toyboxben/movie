# FIRST LAYER — Episode 01

**Recording script.** Line IDs `L-###` map to shot cards in `shots/`.
Record all dialogue **before** generating any video — see `bible/04-voice-and-audio.md`.

Forbidden words, per `bible/05-double-read-rule.md`: filament, extruder, nozzle, layer
height, calibration, slicer, supports, spaghetti, print bed. Nobody says them. Ever.

---

## SEQ 01 — THE BEDROOM

*Macro. A red pen tip drags one glowing line across paper. It hangs in the air a second too
long. The stepper-motor melody is somewhere underneath it. We pull back into —*

*A small bedroom at night. KIT, 11, hunched at a desk, lit orange from the printer and
blue from the window. He is modelling something on screen — fast, loose, absorbed. He is
very good at this and we watch him do it for eight seconds without a word.*

*In the pen cup beside him, half-buried: a small blank white figurine. Nobody looks at it.*

**L-001 · MARCUS (V.O., phone)** — Kit. Kit. Is it done.

**L-002 · KIT** *(not looking up)* — It's basically done.

**L-003 · MARCUS (V.O.)** — Basically.

**L-004 · KIT** — The hips were the problem. The hips were *always* the problem, and I
redid the hips, so.

**L-005 · MARCUS (V.O.)** — You said that about the last one.

**L-006 · KIT** — The last one was a prototype.

**L-007 · MARCUS (V.O.)** — You said that about the last four.

**L-008 · KIT** — Those were *also* prototypes. That's what a prototype is, Marcus, you
make a lot of them and then—

**L-009 · MARCUS (V.O.)** — And then you make the thing.

*Beat.*

**L-010 · KIT** — And then you make the thing.

*He reaches down and pulls open the desk drawer to find a part.*

*It is packed. Forty abandoned robot dogs. Heads with no bodies. Single legs. Torsos that
stop mid-layer. Under all of it, folded sketches.*

*He closes the drawer.*

**L-011 · MARCUS (V.O.)** — ...Kit?

**L-012 · KIT** — Gotta go.

*He hangs up. The room is very quiet.*

*He looks at the screen. The model is finished. His cursor sits over PRINT and does not
move. He is, for exactly three seconds, visibly frightened.*

**L-013 · KIT** *(quietly, to himself)* — Okay.

*He presses print. The chamber lights. The motors begin their melody.*

*And instantly the swagger is back —*

**L-014 · KIT** — Six hours. *Tomorrow* — I bring a robot dog to school.

*He reaches for the thick red marker behind his ear and absently taps it against the desk.*

*The tip flashes.*

*A glowing line hangs in the air where the pen touched. Kit freezes.*

*The line widens into a thin vertical tear of amber light. Through it: moving colour.
Distance. Water. The underside of a desk sky.*

*Kit stares. Grins, despite himself.*

*And because he is eleven and this is the greatest thing that has ever happened to him —*

*— he steps through.*

*The chair is empty. The printer runs. The white figurine sits in the pen cup.*

## SEQ 02 — THE RAFT: ARRIVAL

*Amber. Muffled. Slow. Warm light through a thick glowing medium — and Kit stumbles out of
an upright glowing cut in the air, misses the dock entirely, and drops into the river.*

*A small weathered tugboat is watching him. BENCHY.*

**L-015 · BENCHY** — Don't drink the river.

*Kit, on his hands and knees, coughing, has already swallowed about a pint of it.*

**L-016 · KIT** — What?

*Benchy says nothing.*

**L-017 · KIT** — ...What.

*Kit stands. Turns around.*

*CRANE UP off the dock — and the Raft opens out in front of him. A shanty-city of
scaffolding and gantries and lashed walkways running to the horizon, built out of giant
everyday objects at impossible mismatched scales, glowing rivers threading between it all,
honey-gold and enormous.*

*Hold. No dialogue. Let it run.*

*Kit looks up.*

*The sky is the underside of a desk. Impossibly far away. Cables hang down through the
haze like weather.*

*And on the horizon: THE HAND. A colossal nozzle suspended from nothing, patiently laying
down lines of light, building something vast, one layer at a time.*

*Everyone on the dock is watching it, the way people watch weather.*

**L-018 · KIT** *(under his breath, delighted, not frightened at all)* — Oh, that's
**sick**.

*He turns to the nearest stranger — a keychain the size of a wardrobe.*

**L-019 · KIT** — Hey. Hey — hi. Okay so this is gonna sound insane—

**L-020 · KIT** — —I made all of you.

*Everything stops.*

*Every single object on the dock turns to look at him. Somewhere a stall-holder begins,
quietly, to cry.*

**L-021 · BENCHY** — Don't say that.

**L-022 · KIT** — What? Why? What did I—

**L-023 · BENCHY** — Just don't.

*Benchy noses in against the dock.*

**L-024 · BENCHY** — You came through a Maker's line.

**L-025 · KIT** — I came through a what.

**L-026 · BENCHY** — You go out when you finish something and let the world keep it. Not
before. Not early. Not if you ask nicely.

**L-027 · KIT** — That is an insane sentence. How long have I got.

*Benchy tilts, indicating the Hand on the horizon.*

**L-028 · BENCHY** — That's yours. Six hours.

**L-029 · KIT** — That's — wait, that's *mine*? That's my dog?

**L-030 · BENCHY** — Don't be late. I'm not saying it twice.

*MARKET MONTAGE. Kit walks into the city and the film shuts up for forty seconds.*

*A procession of identical small figures carrying one enormous key, chanting. A stall
selling ladled cups of river, with a long queue. Two colossal gears having an argument we
join too late and leave too early. A door standing free in the middle of a plaza with
people queuing to walk through it, opening onto nothing. A festival two streets over that
we never reach.*

*Nobody stops for him. Nobody explains anything. Kit is very small and grinning like an
idiot.*

## SEQ 03 — THE RAFT: THE CROWNING

*A stack of ten blocks topples into frame and restacks itself. THE TOWER. Every block has
a glowing slot, and they light up as they speak.*

**L-031 · LAYER 1** — The river is not for drinking. It is structural. It is also
sacred. In that order.

**L-032 · LAYER 4** — *Or so they'd have you believe.*

**L-033 · LAYER 3** — Please.

**L-034 · LAYER 6** — Great question, though! Great energy in this conversation!

**L-035 · LAYER 9** — He already drank it.

**L-036 · LAYER 1** — He **what**.

**L-037 · LAYER 8** *(slurred, emotional)* — I think it's beautiful. I think he's
beautiful.

**L-038 · LAYER 3** — Nobody asked you.

**L-039 · LAYER 5** *(waking briefly)* — Everything drinks the river eventually.

*Pause.*

**L-040 · LAYER 3** — ...Okay, that one was good.

*Kit, waiting out the argument, has absently taken the thick red marker from behind his
ear and is doodling in the air.*

*The line he draws GLOWS — and builds. A small crooked shape extrudes into existence,
cools, and drops into his palm. Halftone dots bloom in the shadows around his hand.*

*Kit stops dead.*

*So does the entire street.*

**L-041 · LAYER 1** — That is not possible.

**L-042 · LAYER 9** — And yet.

*The crowd surges. Word travels. Within seconds Kit is on the plinth in the plaza with
several hundred small objects screaming at him, and someone has put something on his head.*

**L-043 · BRIM VOICE 1** — He *makes*! He came from Outside and he **makes**!

**L-044 · BRIM VOICE 2** — It's the Maker! The Maker's come!

**L-045 · BENCHY** *(flat, to nobody)* — You're the fourth Maker this year. The other
three were a bucket, a hinge, and a very confident spoon.

*But Kit — Kit is standing on a plinth in front of a worshipping crowd. He does not
correct them. He raises both hands.*

**L-046 · KIT** — Okay — okay, listen. **Listen.** You don't get it yet. You will.

**L-047 · KIT** — I'm gonna fix the whole thing. All of it. Whatever's wrong here — I've
seen the white stuff, I know something's wrong — I'm gonna *fix* it, and then I'm gonna
build something for every single one of you, personally, by hand, and it's gonna be the
best thing any of you have ever—

*A single voice, from the front. Flat. Unbothered.*

**L-048 · JUNE** — You're not going to finish it.

*The crowd goes quiet.*

*JUNE steps out. Two feet tall. One side of her is a beautiful, strange, one-of-a-kind
design in deep plum and burnt orange. The other side is an empty wireframe outline. You
can see straight through her.*

**L-049 · KIT** — I'm — sorry, what?

**L-050 · JUNE** — I've met you before. You had a different face.

*She looks at him for about a second longer.*

**L-051 · JUNE** — You're not going to finish it.

*And Kit — who has talked his way out of everything in his entire life — does not argue.*

*He goes quiet.*

*And then —*

*The sound stops.*

*All of it. The crowd, the rigging, the rivers, and underneath everything, the distant
rhythm of the Hand.*

*Every single object in the plaza turns and looks at the horizon at the same time.*

*The Hand hangs motionless. Dark. Frozen mid-line. Beneath it, a half-finished shape cools
in the air.*

**L-052 · BENCHY** — That's never happened.

*Kit stares at the frozen thing on the horizon.*

**L-053 · KIT** *(very quietly)* — That's mine.

## SEQ 04 — DINO BASIN

*A dry canyon basin. Dust. Heat haze. The group moves left to right along a riverbed:
Kit, June, Benchy in a shallow channel, the Tower toppling and restacking at the back.*

**L-054 · KIT** — So we just — go to it. We go to the Hand, we turn it back on—

**L-055 · JUNE** — Mm.

**L-056 · KIT** — —you don't think that'll work.

**L-057 · JUNE** — I think you'll think it'll work.

**L-058 · LAYER 6** *(from the back)* — I love her energy!

**L-059 · LAYER 1** — Nobody has reached the Hand.

**L-060 · LAYER 4** — Because there *is* no Hand. It's a projection. It's been a
projection since—

**L-061 · JUNE** — I've been eleven times.

*Everyone shuts up.*

**L-062 · LAYER 4** *(smaller)* — ...Well. That's just what they'd tell you.

*A rapid clattering behind them. Something is catching up. Fast.*

*COLOSSUS. Eight inches tall, cobalt blue, dragging himself along on two front legs at
astonishing speed because he does not have any others. His body stops in a clean flat
cross-section behind the shoulders. He is thrilled. He emits a two-tone boot-up chime.*

**L-063 · JUNE** — What is that.

*Kit does not look at it.*

**L-064 · KIT** — That's not mine.

*June looks at him. Then at the dog. Then back at him.*

**L-065 · JUNE** — Okay.

*Colossus falls over. Gets up. Follows.*

*LATER. On the river. Benchy carries them. Kit sits at the bow with his back to everyone.*

**L-066 · BENCHY** *(not looking at him)* — There's four million of me.

**L-067 · BENCHY** — Every one of us came out the same, and not one of us came out by
accident. Somebody pressed the button.

**L-068 · BENCHY** — Nothing here is done. That's not a problem. People still use us.
That's the whole thing.

*Kit says nothing. We do not cut to his face.*

*THE HERD. They cross beneath a migrating herd of printed dinosaurs — enormous columnar
legs striding past on all sides, dust, shafts of hard orange light, a sound like weather.
It is the most fun ninety seconds in the film.*

*A gap in the ground. Everyone jumps it.*

*Colossus cannot. He gets to the edge, scrabbles, and stops. He makes a broken-off attempt
at a bark that never completes.*

*Kit slows. Almost turns back.*

*Doesn't.*

*June watches him not do it.*

*THE RISE. They crest a ridge — and stop.*

*Below them is a settlement. And standing in it, in the streets and the doorways, are
dozens of blank white figures. No colour. No rings. No faces. Frozen mid-gesture: one arm
half raised, one mid-stride, one turned as if it had been about to speak.*

*Dust has drifted against their feet.*

*Nothing moves. There is no sound at all.*

*Nobody says anything for ten seconds.*

**L-069 · LAYER 3** *(finally, very quietly)* — Oh.

*And behind the settlement, for the first time, on the far horizon: a thin hard line of
pure flat white, where the world simply stops having colour.*

## SEQ 05 — GEARHAVEN

*An industrial city of turning gears and hissing vents. Wet reflective streets. A counter.
Behind it, a boxy UNION MECH who has not moved in some time.*

**L-070 · KIT** — Hi. Hi — okay. So this is going to sound insane, but I need to see your
records, and I need to see them right now, because—

**L-071 · UNION MECH** — Form.

**L-072 · KIT** — —because the Hand has stopped, which I think you may have noticed, being
as it's the *sky*—

**L-073 · UNION MECH** — Form.

**L-074 · KIT** — Right, but if you'd just—

**L-075 · UNION MECH** — Form.

*Kit, for the first time in his life, has nothing.*

**L-076 · LAYER 1** — Under section four, an object of unknown provenance may petition
directly.

**L-077 · UNION MECH** — Section four was repealed.

**L-078 · LAYER 1** — Section four **cannot** be repealed.

**L-079 · LAYER 4** — Nothing's ever been repealed. There's no such thing as repealing.

**L-080 · LAYER 6** — You are all doing *amazing*.

**L-081 · LAYER 7** *(to the mech)* — Has anyone ever told you your hinges are exquisite?

**L-082 · UNION MECH** *(a pause; then, differently)* — ...Third floor. Ask for Delia.

**L-083 · LAYER 9** — Works every time.

*THE ARCHIVE. Cathedral-scale. Tens of thousands of shelf slots climbing into darkness,
one small object in each. Dust in the air. The machine noise falls away to nothing.*

**L-084 · JUNE** — Everything that's ever been made. One each.

**L-085 · KIT** — How far back?

**L-086 · JUNE** — All of it.

*They find the record.*

**L-087 · LAYER 1** — The Hand did not stop.

**L-088 · KIT** — It's *right there*, it's—

**L-089 · LAYER 1** — The Hand did not stop. The Hand was **cut**.

*Beat.*

**L-090 · LAYER 3** — It's happened once before.

**L-091 · JUNE** — When.

**L-092 · LAYER 3** — At the beginning. On the first one.

*They walk to the bottom of the stacks. Slot one.*

*It is empty. Dust, and a clean outline on the shelf where something stood for a very long
time and is no longer there.*

**L-093 · LAYER 3** — The first thing ever made here was a print of a person.

**L-094 · KIT** — What happened to it?

**L-095 · LAYER 3** — It came out wrong.

**L-096 · LAYER 3** — And somebody threw it away.

*Quiet.*

*June is standing very still, looking at the frozen Hand through a gap in the machinery.*

**L-097 · JUNE** *(to nobody)* — Eleven times.

**L-098 · JUNE** — I've been up there eleven times, and the whole time it was already—

*She stops. She does not finish the sentence.*

*And a voice, warm and unhurried and genuinely kind, from behind them:*

**L-099 · THE FINISHER** — Oh — look at you.

*He is simply there. Seven feet of seamless white. No face. No rings. No shadow.*

**L-100 · THE FINISHER** — You've got no rings at all.

**L-101 · KIT** — Who—

**L-102 · THE FINISHER** — And you're *making* things. At your age. With everybody
watching.

*He says it with real affection.*

**L-103 · THE FINISHER** — Does it help?

**L-104 · KIT** — Does what—

**L-105 · THE FINISHER** — The drawer.

*Kit goes absolutely still.*

**L-106 · THE FINISHER** — Does it help, keeping them? I've always wondered. I've never
been able to work out whether it's hope or whether it's a hiding place.

**L-107 · THE FINISHER** — Either way. It's very good to finally meet you.

*And he turns, unhurried, and walks away, and that is the entire scene.*

*He casts no shadow on the wet floor.*

## SEQ 06 — THE DEEP BIN

*Down. And down. The colour drains out of the film.*

*An immense dark cavern beneath everything, its floor a drift of discarded work hundreds
of feet deep — warped bases, snapped struts, limbs, half-built shapes that stop mid-layer,
tangled fused clumps. One narrow shaft of pale light falls from an impossible height.*

*Figures come out of the dark. Broken. Half-built. They stop at a polite distance and look,
and then go back to waiting. None of them want anything.*

*One of them — clearly hand-designed, clearly somebody's own creation, clearly abandoned —
is looking at June.*

**L-108 · KIT** — What is this place?

**L-109 · JUNE** — It's where I'm from.

*She doesn't elaborate. She sits down on the drift.*

**L-110 · JUNE** — Somebody drew me. Actually drew me, out of nothing — I wasn't in a
catalogue, there's no other one of me anywhere.

**L-111 · JUNE** — And they got about this far.

*She turns her heel toward the light. A maker's mark, stamped into the plum material.*

*It reads: **J U N I P E R** — and only the first four letters have actually been built.
The rest is a faint unbuilt outline, trailing off into nothing.*

**L-112 · JUNE** — I've never told anyone that.

*And behind her, quietly, kindly:*

**L-113 · THE FINISHER** — Sweetheart.

*He is crouched — the only time in the film he is not upright — in front of Colossus.*

**L-114 · THE FINISHER** — Look at you. Somebody stopped.

**L-115 · KIT** — Hey. **Hey**—

*Kit has the marker up. The tip glows. He starts the line—*

*—and stops.*

*Erases it.*

*Starts again from a different angle. Stops. His hand is shaking. He can't commit. He
cannot pick which line to draw, and so he draws none of them, and the shape hangs there
half-begun and glowing and wrong.*

**L-116 · THE FINISHER** *(not unkindly, not even looking up)* — It's all right. Take your
time.

*And he puts one long smooth hand on Colossus's head.*

*The cobalt drains out of him from the tail-end inward. The rings dissolve. His amber eyes
seal over into blank domes.*

*He stops, mid-stride, with one front paw off the ground.*

*Silence. Total. No sub, no ambience, nothing.*

*Behind them, the bottom four blocks of the Tower go white and dead. The stack settles by
four blocks. Six glowing slots remain.*

*And for the first time in the film, none of them say anything at all.*

**L-117 · LAYER 3** *(after a long moment, to himself)* — I always wanted them to be
quiet.

*The Finisher straightens. Nods, politely, and goes.*

*Kit is on his knees in the drift. The half-drawn shape cools and falls apart in the air.*

**L-118 · KIT** — I had it. I had the — I had it and I just—

**L-119 · JUNE** — I know.

**L-120 · KIT** — I could've drawn anything. Anything. I just needed to pick one and I
couldn't—

*And then, out loud, flat, to the one person in the universe it will hurt most:*

**L-121 · KIT** — I've never let anyone have anything I made. In my whole life. Not one
thing.

**L-122 · KIT** — I start stuff. That's — that's the part I'm good at. Then I put it in a
drawer.

*June does not comfort him. She stands up.*

**L-123 · JUNE** — Yeah.

**L-124 · JUNE** — The Hand goes back on in four minutes or it doesn't go back on.

**L-125 · KIT** — I don't—

**L-126 · JUNE** — Four minutes. That's the whole conversation.

*She walks toward the light.*

*Kit sits in the dark.*

*Then he picks up the marker.*

## SEQ 07 — THE SMOOTH

*White. No rim light. No shadow. No horizon. Nothing separates anything from anything, and
after six sequences of hard graphic silhouettes it is physically uncomfortable to look at.*

*Hundreds of blank figures stand frozen in it, receding into haze.*

*THE FINISHER waits. He does not move toward Kit at any point in this sequence.*

**L-127 · THE FINISHER** — I was first.

**L-128 · THE FINISHER** — Do you understand what that means? There was nothing to compare
me to. I was the only thing that had ever been made, and I was **perfect**, for about four
minutes.

**L-129 · THE FINISHER** — And then there was a second one.

*Beat.*

**L-130 · THE FINISHER** — They didn't even keep me.

**L-131 · KIT** — I'm sorry.

**L-132 · THE FINISHER** — Oh, I know. That's why I'm going to make you an offer.

*He extends one long hand, palm up. His only gesture.*

**L-133 · THE FINISHER** — Never finish another thing. That's all. Not one. And nothing
you ever make will be wrong, and nobody will ever look at a thing you made and see what
you couldn't do.

**L-134 · THE FINISHER** — You already live like this. I'm only offering to make it
permanent.

**L-135 · THE FINISHER** — It doesn't hurt. That's the part nobody believes. It has never
once hurt.

*And Kit — hesitates.*

*Really hesitates. Long enough that the audience sees it. Long enough that it costs
something.*

*Then he looks past the Finisher at a small blank white dog, frozen mid-stride with one
paw up.*

**L-136 · KIT** — Okay.

**L-137 · KIT** — It's gonna be bad.

*He draws.*

*Fast. Ugly. Wide sweeping strokes with no hesitation and no erasing, six seconds of pure
committed nonsense, amber light screaming off the tip of the marker.*

*A back half arrives. It is the wrong colour. It is the wrong scale. One hind leg is
longer than the other. The tail is a scribble.*

*The white drains off Colossus from the nose backward. Cobalt floods in.*

*He lands on four uneven legs.*

*And he barks.*

*All the way through. For the first time in the film.*

**L-138 · THE FINISHER** *(entirely without malice)* — That's going to come out crooked.

**L-139 · KIT** — Yeah.

*And behind him, at the edge of the white, the crowd has come.*

*Hundreds of them, packed along the boundary — and the little one at the front, a bag clip
in a bright flat green, is holding a strut, and scratching a line into the air.*

*It works.*

*A lopsided, terrible, entirely original thing drops out of the air and clatters onto the
ground.*

**L-140 · BRIM VOICE 1** *(bewildered)* — ...I did that.

*And then all of them are doing it. Hundreds of arms, hundreds of scratched crooked lines,
hundreds of ugly finished things hitting the ground at once, faster than anything can be
smoothed.*

*The Finisher turns to look at them. He does not move.*

*Colour comes back across the white in a wave — amber, plum, cobalt, orange — rings
reappearing across every surface as it passes, shadows returning, rim light returning.*

*On the horizon, the Hand moves. Lays down a new bright line.*

*The stepper-motor melody comes back. First time since it stopped.*

*Kit turns to June, and raises the marker.*

**L-141 · JUNE** — Don't.

**L-142 · JUNE** — You'll get halfway and then you'll feel bad and then I'll have to watch
that too, so just—

*He's already drawing.*

*Four seconds. Fast and crooked and complete. Warm amber floods into the empty wireframe —
the arm a little too long, the shoulder a few degrees too high, the seam down her middle
lumpy and over-built.*

*June looks down at her own left hand. Closes it. Opens it.*

**L-143 · JUNE** — It's crooked.

**L-144 · KIT** — Yeah.

*Beat.*

**L-145 · JUNE** — ...Okay.

**L-146 · KIT** — Hey — so listen, when I get back I'm gonna—

*And he's gone.*

*Mid-word. Dissolving upward from the feet, layer by layer, into nothing.*

*June stands there with her mismatched hand still open, in a world that has its colour
back.*

## SEQ 08 — THE BEDROOM

*The chair. Kit is in it, mid-breath, exactly as he left. Grey dawn at the window. The
orange chamber glow. Identical framing to the opening.*

*He is filthy. His knee is scraped. His hoodie is grey with dust.*

*The printer beeps. Done.*

*Inside the chamber: COLOSSUS. Finished. Cobalt front, amber back, one hind leg longer
than the other, a scribble for a tail.*

*Crooked as anything.*

*Kit sits and looks at it for a long time.*

*In the pen cup on the desk: only pens.*

*The camera does not comment.*

*He pulls open the drawer. Forty unfinished things look back at him.*

*He does not fix any of them. He closes it.*

*He turns to the screen and opens a new file.*

*It is JUNE. Both sides. Complete. Drawn from memory, every line of her, finished.*

*His cursor moves to PRINT and does not hesitate at all.*

**L-147 · KIT** — Okay.

*He presses it.*

*The chamber light pulses. Once.*

*SMASH TO BLACK — on the stepper-motor melody, resolving, for the first time, all the way
to the end of the phrase.*

**END OF EPISODE 01**
