# The Double Read Rule

**Nobody needs to know anything about 3D printing to love this film.**

The printing material is the film's texture, its mythology, and its gift to the people who
do know — but it can never be a prerequisite. The rule is simple and absolute:

> Every insider reference must have a plain-language reading that is just as good, and the
> jargon word is never spoken aloud.

A newcomer reads the world as a striking design language. A person who owns a printer
gasps roughly once a minute. Neither audience is asked to work.

---

## The translation table

| The thing | Insider read | Newcomer read | Word we actually use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Layer lines | layer lines | everyone is built from stacked rings, like tree rings | **rings** |
| Filament | filament | glowing molten stuff the rivers are made of | **the river**, **the glow** |
| The extruder / nozzle | the print head | a colossal hand in the sky that builds the world | **the Hand** |
| A Benchy | *the* universal calibration boat | a grumpy little tugboat | **Benchy** (just his name) |
| Temperature tower | a temp calibration stack | a stack of blocks that argue with each other | **the Tower** |
| Failed print / spaghetti | spaghetti | a melted mess | **a snarl** |
| Support structures | supports | scaffolding that gets snapped off and binned | **props** |
| Vapor smoothing | vapor smoothing | he rubs the rings off you until you're blank | **smoothing** |
| Print bed | the bed | the ground itself, warm and glassy | **the Bed** |
| Stringing | stringing | wispy threads trailing off things | **wisps** |
| First layer | the critical first layer | the thing everything else is built on | **the first layer** |

**Forbidden in dialogue, forever:** filament, extruder, nozzle, PLA, layer height,
calibration, slicer, adhesion, retraction, overhang, print bed, supports, spaghetti.

Those words are welcome in prompts, in this repo, and in the art. They are never in a
character's mouth.

---

## How the mythology reads to a newcomer

Strip the printing out entirely and this is still a complete fantasy world:

- A world of living toys, built from stacked rings, who consider those rings a birthmark
- Rivers of warm glowing liquid that everything is made from
- A giant hand in the sky that builds new life, one layer at a time, and has never stopped
- A sky that is the underside of a desk, and weather that is somebody's bedroom
- A faceless smooth man with no rings who thinks being visibly imperfect is a form of pain

Not one of those requires a printer. All of them are better if you have one.

---

## The bonus layer, for the people who know

These land silently. Never point at them.

- Benchy is the first thing every printer makes, so he has met four million of himself.
- The Tower's ten personalities run cold-and-brittle at the bottom to hot-and-slurring at
  the top, in the correct order, at the correct temperatures.
- The Bin-Lost are warped bases, elephant's foot, snapped overhangs and fused snarls.
- The Finisher's total lack of texture is the uncanny thing about him, and printing people
  will feel it before they can name it.
- The Hand has always been building whatever the visitor is printing. Which is why it is
  building a dog.

---

## The test

Before any scene ships, ask: **would this play for a nine-year-old who has never seen a
printer?**

If the answer depends on the audience knowing what a support structure is, rewrite the
line. If a character has to explain a printing concept for a joke to work, the joke is
cut. The Tower is allowed to explain *world* rules — never *printing* rules.
