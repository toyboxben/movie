# Draftlands — TEMP script: first ~2 minutes

> **TEMPORARY.** Curiosity draft only — not locked.  
> Covers pilot cold open **A1→A5** compressed into ~2:00.  
> Rule: show the machine before anyone names it. Almost no jargon.  
> Quill / Mira locked. Companion unnamed.

---

```
DRAFTLANDS
"Cold Open" — temp pages
Approx. 0:00–2:00

FADE IN:

EXT. HIGH LUNA STREET — DAY (thin pale light)

Clean stone. Repeated architecture — the SAME window shape twice in one shot.
A small pressed PET FORM trots past... and its twin sits on a stoop. Same face. Same stitch.

Amber dust motes hang lazy in the air. Thin.

A VENDOR (gloved hands — no gold) opens a clean plate-book.
Lays it flat.

A CLEAN CUBE blooms on the stoop like origami deciding to exist.
Fog packs inside — familiar silhouette.
The shell sheds.
A helper-Form steps out. Ordinary. Nobody applauds.

VENDOR (optional, almost bored)
Stamp’s ready.

People take it like weather they trust.

CAMERA FINDS — farther down the street —

A TARP.
Wrong shape underneath. Breathing. Packing sounds. Not a cube.

QUILL (15–16) kneels beside it.
Academy coat. Crest. Bound book open on his knees.
His fingertips are GOLD — stained, unwashed.
Most hands on this street are clean or gloved. His are not.

A crooked GOLD LINE lifts from the page as a ribbon —
feeds into the tarp ropes like IV light.

Under the tarp edge: one CLEAR EYE. Lopsided. Alive. His.

He mutters to it — not a conversation. Corrections. Care.

QUILL
(under breath)
Stay. Stay in.

WARDEN BELL — distant. Hard.

The sky BRUISES. Amber motes THICKEN fast.
Shutters slam. Cubes get dragged indoors.
People move like they’ve done this drill.

A voice, half-heard, from somewhere up-street:

VOICE (O.S.)
Strong Draft—!

MIRA hits frame — grabs Quill’s coat.

MIRA
Inside. Now.

He looks at the tarp.
The eye looks back.
No plate. No cube. No “stamp’s ready.”

QUILL
It doesn’t have—

MIRA
I don’t care.

The wind RISES. Potential goes WHITE-GOLD.
A Warden sail deploys at the far end of the street — imperfect, straining.

Quill stands.
Book open.
Ribbon shaking in his gold hand.
Teenage certainty.

He RUNS INTO the thickening bloom —
against the crowd.

Mira’s face — the first wound — as he leaves her.

INT./EXT. THE BLOOM — CONTINUOUS

The street REWRITES while he runs.
Scaffold grows through a doorway.
A clean cube mid-stamp OVERWRITES into something hungrier.

Quill drops to the tarp.
Forces the ribbon into a HOLDING SEAL —
gold stitches slamming shut around the wrong silhouette.

QUILL
I’ve got you—!

The Draft FINDS the sticky Mark.
Treats his crooked companion-line like a PLATE.

The STREET starts pressing copies of HIS line —
walls, shutters, even the air taking the lopsided eye.

The tarp RIPS.

The companion surges — huge, wrong, loved —
and the district begins to become IT.

Quill’s face: not triumph. Horror.

He grabs the book.
Gold light everywhere.
People scream his direction — at the gold hands.

He RUNS —
shame first, then survival —
into the whiteout.

CUT TO BLACK.

TITLE (maybe later):
DRAFTLANDS

END TEMP COLD OPEN (~2:00)
```

## What this is trying to teach (without saying it)

| Picture | Watcher should feel |
|---------|---------------------|
| Cube → fog → known Form | Making here is tidy / catalog-safe |
| Twin pets / repeated windows | Things come from plates |
| Gold fingers + crooked ribbon + tarp | Quill’s making is different / personal / off-track |
| Bell + thickening dust + “Strong Draft” | Draft = weather of becoming; danger |
| Mira pulls him back; he goes anyway | Relationship + stubborn love |
| Sticky Mark → street becomes his line | Disaster = his Freeline caught by the storm |
| Flee | Guilt engine for the season |
