## LOCKED (do not reopen without an explicit decision)

1. **Potential** — name of the ambient dust / field of becoming.
2. **Knowledge split** — everyone knows Potential exists early (like wind). **Citizens / Quill / audience** do not start knowing the ecology: making circulates it; stillness / Safe-Only starve it. That circulation rule is the season discovery for hero + audience. Do **not** treat Potential itself as a mid-season secret reveal. **Lean refinement:** High Luna Wardens / academy elders may **already know** (or strongly suspect) and suppress it — see LOCKED 17.
3. **Otherwhere / uploads** — not villain cargo. 3rd-party / shared Forms count as making. “From-scratch purity” is antagonist ideology; the show argues remix + share + build-together.
4. **Resolve** — the *act* of packing a Mark into a Form. Never also the dust name.
5. **Toybox** — not a character / mascot; at most inferred (Outside / Oybo).
6. **Not everyone is a full-time Maker** — making is ambient culture, not universal job title. Wardens, traders, kids, people who mostly *use* Forms, etc.

6b. **Modalities are specialties, not castes** — technically anyone can Stack, Freeline, Craftbound, or pull Otherwhere. People have a **preference and specialty**; labels (Freeliner, Stacker, Indexer…) describe how you usually make, not what you’re allowed to touch. Gatekeeping after the disaster is politics, not physics.
7. **Post-disaster freeze is scoped** — after the hero’s disaster, home does **not** stop all making. **Freeliners** are banned, shamed, or driven underground. Stackers + Craftbound keep running. The city looks orderly and “safe” while Potential still thins — because the messy channel that circulates the field hardest was cut.
8. **Freeliners / Freelining** — freehand / slate / draw-speak-resolve specialty (was Gestureborn). Not `-born`. **Freelining is unpredictable** — that volatility is why **High Luna / Safe-Only** fear it and ban it after the disaster. Working lean: folk Draftlands treat Freeline as normal art; elite rules are class law, not physics. Reliability + power issue, not blood caste.
9. **Stack vocabulary** — catalog clean-cube culture. Formal: **Stackers** / **From the Stack**. Slang/roles: **Indexers**, **Cubers**, **Stampers** (the shell and/or the operator). Working (not locked): **plates** = originals in the library; **presses** = copy plates indefinitely; **Indexing** = Luna-gated act of turning a Mark into a plate. Do not call a press a Stamper.
10. **Hero age lane** — mid-teen (~15–16), Eren Yeager altitude: intensity / guilt-rage / vow weight. Not a little kid.
11. **Art direction** — Higgsfield *Kök Börü* / Soul Cartoon **rendering language** (not that film’s story, steppe, snow, or wardrobe). Hybrid 2D+3D; bold visible brushstrokes; painted textures; expressive faces; simplified graphic Forms (planar / carved, not photoreal CG). Dual-audience, not preschool. Recurring locations stay in this lock.
12. **Lead names** — protagonist **Quill**; tether **Mira**.
13. **Gold fingers** — Freeliner tell. Potential gold stains the hands (often fingertips/cuticles) and does not fully wash out. Prestige mark in academy Freeline lanes; stigma after Safe-Only. Gloves, ink, or Stack-clean hands become political. Quill’s gold is visible in Act 1; he cannot scrub the identity off in exile.
14. **Living Forms (working → lean lock):** Animate / companion Forms are **normal**, not Quill’s invention. Stack streets already have pressed pets, helpers, walking tools from life-plates. What is rare / policed: **unplated Freeline life** — a one-off living Mark that Luna won’t Index. Academy may allow student Freeline on inert Forms first; living Holds are licensed, watched, or “pull it from the plates.” Quill’s tarp companion is special because it is **his, crooked, and unbacked** — not because life itself is forbidden or unique.
15. **Warden Freeline monopoly (lean lock):** Citizen sermon = Freeline forbidden / stamp-only. Academy still **teaches Freeline** to make Wardens. Ban = “you don’t; we do.” Quill’s sin is off-track Freeline (street / unplated life), not the existence of Freeline class.
16. **Class / ultra-wealth theme (lean lock):** Season critiques elite monopoly on creation. High Luna ultra-wealth / academy / Warden class hoards Freeline (and plates), sells “safety” as stamp-only citizenship, blames lower cities for Freeline “ruins,” while Potential dies in the tidy city. Quill’s arc is privilege → cast out → learn folk making → return authorship to the street — not bootstraps worship, not “eat the rich” PSA. Show the class machine; don’t speech it.
17. **Kings of ruins (lean lock):** Wardens / academy power know (or choose to know) that Potential dries when people stop making. They still fence Freeline and push stamp-only citizenship — **willing to rule ruins rather than share the means of making.** Power over a dying field beats a living commons. Not ignorance; monopoly. Quill learns ecology from folk first (making feeds Potential). He returns as messenger. City won’t listen, **then** he learns they knew — rage. Reveal vehicles: ledger, elder slip, Warden confession, Mira’s proof — after refusal, not before.

## Core premise (working)

An endless, contrasting world of constant becoming — themed regions, chaos, things coming into existence. No one in-world knows where existence comes from (myth fragments only). **Potential** (the ambient medium of becoming) is thinning. That scarcity causes **worse drafts** in some places and **spreading deserts** in others. When societies outlaw or fear **messy making** (Freeliners) and retreat to only “safe” Stack patterns, the field starves further. The story argues — without speeches — that **we must keep building, including the dangerous kind.**

## World physics (felt, not explained)

### Drafts

- Massive **weather-scale** events of becoming
- Can be soft (gentler births) or **bad** (destructive creation: new things spawn by ripping through / overwriting what exists)
- Calendar, lore, drama, set pieces
- Never fully explained cosmologically

### Potential — LOCKED

- Always a little in the air (Dune-spice ecology + Star Wars Force feel)
- Thick in storms / blooms
- Absent in deserts / Quiet / Stillsky / Grainless reaches → creation famine
- Harvestable, political, sacred, contested
- **Living current (season truth):** using/making circulates it; stillness / hoarding / fear dries it
- Optional slang only: Drift, Firstgrain, Motes — never replace the locked name on the page

#### What people know vs what they learn — LOCKED

- **Known early (common knowledge):** Potential is real. You can see it, breathe it, feel when drafts are fat or thin. Deserts mean “no Potential.” Wardens work the weather. Kids know the word the way kids know “wind.”
- **Unknown / wrongly believed early:** *why* it’s thinning, and the loop that **making circulates it / not-making starves it**. Myths blame outsiders, Freeliner “wrongness,” Otherwhere arrivals, or “the world is ending so stop stirring it.”
- **Season discovery (hero + audience together):** the ecology. Stillness and purity-panic dry the field; shared making replenishes it. Potential is **not** a secret element reveal. The **rule of circulation** is the reveal.
- Audience may feel the print metaphor before characters do; characters never need a full cosmology.

### Wardens

- Can **harness / redirect / harden** drafts imperfectly — never fully control
- Levees and sails against a hurricane of becoming
- “For good” is contested (can spill Hard Drafts onto poorer districts)
- Naruto-adjacent readable technique; squad roles possible (Anchor / Cutter / Midwife / Spill)

### Coming into existence

- Mechanism **undetermined** cosmologically in-world; myths contradict
- **Visible birth (working):** most intentional Makes pass through a **Stamper** — see below
- Audience may eventually feel “print volume”; characters never need that word

### Stampers (working mechanics — visible Resolve)

The readable on-screen way a Mark becomes a Form. Not the cosmology. Not called a printer.

**What it looks like**

- A **shell of becoming** — often a **cube or rectilinear volume** for Stack / From the Stack work (clean edges, pale Potential fog inside, soft light).
- Freeliner Stampers can be **irregular** — sketch-shaped, lopsided, cracked, sketchscar-lined — same physics, messier silhouette.
- Otherwhere Stampers may arrive **already mid-fill** or with foreign markings (shared pattern, not blank slate).
- Outside the shell: air thickens with Potential; inside, the Form packs until the shell sheds / dissolves at Resolve.

**Time ≈ complexity (working)**

- **Small / simple** (bolt, cup, little companion) → near-instant or a breath. Kids can Stamper something in a scene without waiting.
- **Medium** → a scene beat / conversation / chase pause.
- **Large / complex** (vehicles, architecture, living multi-part Forms) → long holds; montage, night-to-morning, draft weather as clock.
- Thin Potential → Stampers **stall mid-shell**. Fat drafts → they finish too fast and spill / overwrite.

**By modality (feel)**

- **Stackers:** Index a known pattern → clean cube Stamper → predictable Resolve.
- **Craftbound:** tool/app-shaped Stampers (purpose-built).
- **Freeliners:** Mark first (draw / speak / slate) → Stamper grows to fit the Mark → Resolve. Wrong Marks → wrong shells.
- **Otherwhere:** shell may look “already started” — remix / finish counts as making.

**Story uses**

- Safe-Only streets still full of tidy Stack cubes; messy Freeline shells gone
- Hero’s vow: he can *watch* Stack Stampers; touching Freeliner shells is the relapse

**Language**

- **Stamper** = the shell (and slang for the operator). **Resolve** = the finish. Never “print” on screen.
- **Audience teaching (pilot):** show a clean stamp (cube → fog → known Form) *before* any expulsion speech says “stamp.” Show Quill’s tarp/gold ribbon *before* anyone explains Freeline. Jargon only after the picture.

### Plates, libraries, and presses (working)

Three objects. Do not smash them.

1. **Plates / originals** — the first drawings, kept in libraries. One window, drawn once. Architecture repeats because the city is pressed from a few plates.
2. **Presses** — copy a plate indefinitely into working books. Thud, heat, Potential fog. Respectable. The economy.
3. **Stamper** — the shell that packs a Mark into a Form in the world. The press copies the *page*. The Stamper copies it into *matter*.

**Who makes plates**

- Most people **never** make a plate. They Index one that exists and press it.
- Physics (LOCKED 6b): anyone *can* Freeline a new Mark.
- Politics: turning that Mark into a **plate** — something presses will copy forever — is rare and licensed. **High Luna holds the monopoly** on Indexing. Alpha lives on worn copies exported down.
- Common in-world idea: elders / Luna decide what is allowed to exist more than once. Not “Freeliners are a different species.” Status, money, architecture.
- Alpha Freeliners make **one-offs**. If Luna does not Index them, they die with the person. Stack things can be pressed again. Freeline things cannot, unless plated.

**Story uses**

- City looks repeated = few plates, many presses.
- High Luna = prestige libraries behind glass. Alpha = soft-edged copies.
- Otherwhere = a foreign library arriving already pressed. Remix = drawing on top of a plate.
- Safe-Only after the disaster: libraries still open, presses still running, Freeline books gone.
- Civic-scale fear: a new line in a Hard Draft can be treated as a plate the storm copies across every matching press-run.

## Modalities of making (Toybox stack → in-world cultures)

Do **not** call results “toys.” Prefer: Forms, Makes, Pieces, beings, creations.

> **LOCKED naming:** freehand = **Freeliners**. Catalog culture = **Stackers** / **From the Stack**, with slang **Indexers**, **Cubers**, **Stampers** (shell + operator). `-born` labels retired.


| Toybox real modality        | In-world working name    | Feel                                                                                                          |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Catalog → print             | **The Stack** / Stackers | Retrieve familiar patterns; clean cubes; status vs stagnation; **reliable**                                   |
| Creator Space apps          | **Craftbound**           | Single-purpose guild crafts (Shapers, Softwrights, Gearbinders, Chromers…)                                    |
| AI / finger-draw → fidelity | **Freeliners**           | Freehand Mark → Stamper → Resolve; messy, authorial, **unpredictable** — loved and feared for the same reason |
| Uploads from elsewhere      | **Otherwhere / Intake**  | Forms that arrive already shaped (3rd-party / shared patterns); remix & build-with, not “less real”           |


### Freeliners (explored deepest so far)

- Hero specialty. People who prefer / excel this way are called **Freeliners** — not a closed caste.
- **Core fear (LOCKED among elites / Luna):** Freelining is **unpredictable**. Same Mark can Resolve crooked, overgrow, echo, or catch a draft wrong. Stack is boring because it’s *reliable*; Freeline is exciting because it isn’t.
- **Culture invert (working lean):** For **most people**, Freelining is the **natural art form** — vernacular making, gold fingers common in Alpha streets, tarp projects as normal life. **High Luna** (and academy prestige culture) treats uncontrolled Freeline as dangerous, childish, or low — pushes plates, presses, Index discipline. Rules against Freeline are **class law**, not physics.
- Safe-Only after Quill’s disaster is Luna/academy logic winning: “we can’t afford wild Stampers” — a ban that hurts the folk art hardest while shelves stay Stack-full.
- **Hero tool (working):** a bound **paper book**, not a tablet. Finger draws a gold Potential line on the page; the line **peels off as a ribbon** into the street. Crooked gold sketch-lines, not CAD wireframe, not UI.
- Academy / Stack tools can still be slates or devices (draw, words, speak, imprint). Don’t default the show to glowing screens.
- Can also draw in open **Potential/drift** when thick
- Failures: misresolve, overgrow, echo, split, sticky sketch, draft bleed, **plate-bleed** (Hard Draft treats a sticky Mark as a new original and copies it across matching presses)
- “Wrong” makes carry sketchscars; authorship visible

### Otherwhere / Intake (uploads) — LOCKED

- Real-world map: files / models from **elsewhere** (3rd-party sites, shared libraries) — not invented from a blank slate in front of you.
- Arrivals are **not** the villain. Bringing a Form in and building with it / for others **is making.**
- **Purity ideology (antagonist only):** “Only from-scratch is real. Printing / Resolving someone else’s Mark isn’t creation.”
- **Show thesis (behavior, not PSA):** using what others made, finishing it, combining it, shipping it to people — that *is* creation. Together you make **bigger** things than any solitary “pure” Mark.
- Drama: gatekeepers of purity / isolation after fear — not cursed cargo.
- Optional color: Otherwhere Forms often look “already finished” next to Freeliner sketchscars — difference, not ranking.

### Resolve (act)

- The moment a Mark packs into a finished Form
- Naming note: **Potential** = the field (locked). **Resolve** = the act of packing a Mark into a Form. Do not also name the dust Resolve.


### Living Forms — what’s normal vs what’s Quill’s problem (lean lock)

**People make living things.** This is not robot-forbidden sci-fi and Quill is not the first to animate matter.

| Kind | How common | Read |
|------|------------|------|
| **Stack living Forms** | Everywhere | Catalog companions, helpers, beasts from **life-plates**. Same faces repeated down the street. Press again if the Draft takes them. Safe-Only loves these. |
| **Craftbound living** | Common in guild lanes | Purpose-built animate tools (softwright pets, gearbinders’ walkers, etc.). |
| **Otherwhere living** | Present | Arrivals already “on”; remix/care counts as making. |
| **Freeline living (plated)** | Rare, prestige | Luna Indexes a living Mark into a plate; then it can be pressed. Elite Freeline graduation fantasy. |
| **Freeline living (unplated)** | Uncommon + policed | One-offs under tarps. If the Draft takes them, **gone**. Academy: throw back / don’t Hold life without license. Neighbors side-eye. |

**Quill’s tarp companion is special because:**
- No plate (cannot be pressed again)
- Crooked / not Indexable
- Student Freeline life (privilege + rule-bend)
- He loves it past the rubric

**Not because** life is illegal planet-wide or he’s inventing consciousness.

**Disaster read:** plate-bleed of a *living* wrong Mark is worse than bleeding cups — the street fills with *his* creature. That’s why Safe-Only can ban Freeline hard after.

**Visual grammar:** Act 1 streets should show **some** living Stack Forms (repeated designs) so the audience isn’t shocked that Forms can walk — only shocked that Quill’s under the tarp is singular and wrong.

## Who makes (LOCKED)

- **Not a planet of identical builders.** Most people live *among* Forms. Fewer are full-time Makers.
- **Anyone can use any modality.** Stack, Freeline, Craftbound, Otherwhere — the physics don’t care. What’s common is a **preference and specialty** (training, taste, local culture, tools on hand).
- Labels are soft: a “Stacker” is someone who *usually* pulls from the Stack; they might Freeline a birthday gift. A Freeliner might Index something urgent when freehand would take too long.
- **Stackers** and **Craftbound** feel ordinary, respectable, everywhere — the default economy of making.
- **Freeline** is common as folk practice; **prestige Gatekeeping** makes *licensed / academy Freeline* feel rare and policed. Luna fear: unpredictable. Alpha street: Tuesday. Easier to blame when drafts go wrong — especially if the scapegoat is an academy kid whose Mark plate-bled.
- **Otherwhere** sit beside them; purity panic often hits Freeliners and Otherwhere in the same breath.
- After the disaster, bans are **political** (“you may not Freeline here”), not “Freeliners are a different species.”
- Visual read when home goes bad: shelves still fill with Stack Forms; **messy Freeline shells vanish from the street**. Quiet creativity, not a dead city.

## Season 1 conflict (working root)

**Potential is becoming scarce.**

Symptoms (known early):

- Worse / more vicious drafts in some areas
- More deserts / Quiet spreading

Root (hidden early, revealed gradually):

- Field thinning / bottled / hoarded / unbalanced
- After the hero’s disaster, **his city bans or chokes Freeliner making** (fear + bad law + scapegoating him)
- Stackers/Craftbound continue — so the freeze is believable
- Cutting the messy channel **dries Potential further** → death spiral that looks like “we did the safe thing”

**Villain-shaped ideologies (menu):**

- Freezer (stillness = safety — rare extreme; usually loses to the softer lie below)
- Safe-Only / Anti-Freeliner (“Freelining is unpredictable; it caused the disaster; stay Stack”)
- Purifier / From-Scratchist (only “original” Marks count; Otherwhere and remixes are fake — **show does not side with them**)
- Steward who redirects “for the realm” onto the poor
- Harvester who stockpiles Potential
- Lie: “If we stop the dangerous kind of making, the world stabilizes.”  
- Truth: **hoarding + fear of messy / shared making starve the field; making together — including Freeliners and remix — circulates Potential.**
- Darker truth: some at the top **know** the lie and keep it — better kings of ruins than equals in a living world.

Analogy (never spoken as PSA): a creative economy that only allows catalog-safe output slowly dies; freestyle and sharing keep it alive.

## Protagonist — Quill (breakdown)

### Identity
- **Name (LOCKED):** Quill
- **Age (LOCKED):** ~15–16 (Eren Yeager lane — intensity, not cute-kid)
- **Class (working → lean lock):** **high** — academy track, prestige Freeline lane, the kind of kid who *gets* a book and time to Stamper in the street. Not a street orphan story. Closer to High Luna / gated making culture than Alpha scrap.
- **Specialty:** Academy Freeline student (Warden-track curriculum) — **intense and capable at living/messy Holds**; “not good” by Index/Warden-discipline standards; tarp companion = off-track Freeline (elite access, poor grades, real craft)
- **Home:** prestige / academy city (High Luna–adjacent or Alpha’s gated quarter). Stack is respectable; Freeline-at-academy is flashy privilege until it isn’t.
- **Signature tool:** bound **paper book**. Finger draws a **gold Potential line**; the line peels off the page as a **ribbon** into the world. Crooked gold sketch — never CAD, never UI chrome

### Status trope (LOCKED lane — not literal monarchy)
**Privilege → fall → reclaim** (royal → peasant → throne rhyme, without kings unless we add them later).

| Phase | Status read |
|-------|-------------|
| Belong | “Royal”: academy kid, soft hands, gold-ribbon book, tarp project as *allowed* eccentricity |
| Wound | Stripped: scapegoat of the district disaster; Freelining banned with his name on it |
| Exile | “Peasant”: no academy, no prestige presses, vow, road, deserts, living among people who never had plates |
| Return / climax | “Reclaim throne”: not a crown — he reclaims the *right to make* and becomes the one the city follows back into Freeline. Status restored as courage/authorship, not rank. |

The fall hurts **because he had somewhere to fall from.** Mira can be same class (peer) or bridge (lower / different track) — open.

### Freeline class invert (working lean)
- **Folk (most of Draftlands / Alpha):** Freeline = normal making. Gold fingers ordinary. Tarps on streets. Natural art form.
- **High Luna / academy:** Citizens = stamp-only (sermon). **Academy = Freeline school for future Wardens.** Licensed Freeline behind the wall; street Freeline shamed/illegal. Status + monopoly, not physics (LOCKED 6b).
- **Quill starts Luna-side:** academy Freeline student on the Warden track (or trying to be). Class gold fingers are *earned in school*; his tarp companion is Freeline **without leave** — passion that skips the robe.
- **Exile education:** people who never had plates teach him Freeline as culture/mastery, not stain. Early road: **amazement** — gold fingers everywhere, tarps as Tuesday, living one-offs without Luna license. Complicated: wonder vs his vow vs shame that *this* is what his city called dangerous.
- **Return:** he brings folk Freeline courage into the Luna city — reclaim authorship + lift the class ban’s lie. Soft “army” = makers, not soldiers.
- **Park:** literal conquest army = other story / later season.

### High Luna doctrine vs low-city truth (working → lean lock)

**What they tell citizens (the useful lie):**
- Freelining is dangerous.
- That is why the **lower cities are in ruins**.
- In High Luna, people **Stamp / press from plates** and leave creating to **Wardens** (and/or **elders**).
- Citizens stamp. They do not Freeline. Safety is civilization.

**What is not a contradiction — elite monopoly:**
- Wardens **must** Freeline (harness drafts, midwife Hard weather, author when plates fail). Freeline skill is the job.
- The academy **teaches Freeline** — gated, graded, Index-shaped — as the track toward Warden / elder.
- The ban is not “Freeline doesn’t exist here.” It is “**you** don’t Freeline; **we** do.”
- Public sermon = forbidden. Private curriculum = required for the priesthood of making.
- Quill’s crime shape: Freelining **off-track** — street tarp, unplated living Hold, sticky Mark outside Warden discipline — not “touching a gold line in class.”

**What is actually true (he discovers in the low cities):**
- High Luna has **forgotten how to build** (as a people). Stack-only habit + outsourcing creation to Wardens/elders = a city that consumes plates and does not circulate Potential.
- **Potential is dying** there — tidy shelves, thin air, worse drafts coming.
- Lower cities are not “ruined because they Freeline.” Many are battered by scarcity, Hard Drafts, and Luna neglect/export politics — and they still **know how to make**. Folk Freeline is survival and culture, not the cause of the rot.
- Quill’s amazement: the “ruins” are more alive than Luna’s safe streets.
- **Folk Potential literacy:** commonfolk read / feel the field better than Luna instruments — tapped in through daily Freeline. Academy charts are poorer than a gold-handed street sense.
- Second knife: low-city Freeline isn’t “untrained chaos” — it’s the art Luna hoards under Warden robes and denies to citizens.

**Antagonist truth (reveal on return / mid-late — lean lock):**
- Wardens and academy leadership **knew** Potential thins when the city stops making.
- They did not care enough to share Freeline. Stamp-only + Warden monopoly = **kings of the ruins** — prefer power over a dying field to a living commons they don’t own.
- The lower-cities sermon is cover. Safe-Only after Quill is acceleration of a choice already made.
- Quill’s rage target shifts: not only “I ruined us” → “they chose this.”

**Irony of exile:** they send him (or he flees) toward the proof of their sermon — and the sermon breaks.



### One-liner
A mid-teen **academy** Freeliner who can’t draw “right,” will risk a Hard Draft to save a one-off living companion with no plate, ruins a district when his sticky Mark gets pressed by the storm, vows never to make again — then has to Freeline so his city dares to.

### Want / need / fear
- **Want (external):** Save *his* companion. Keep Freelining on his terms. Later: fix what he broke / be left alone with the guilt.
- **Need (internal):** Stop treating Freeline as academy flourish or shame. Learn it as the living art most people already practice — appreciate it, then **master his own crooked Freeline** (not become Index-perfect). Refusing to make starves everyone, including Mira’s home.
- **Fear:** That he is what adults say — dangerous, unfocused, a walking Hard Draft. After the wound: that every Mark he draws will plate-bleed again.
- **Lie he believes:** “My Freeline making destroys. If I stop, the world is safer.”
- **Truth the season proves:** “Their ban on messy making destroys deeper. I must make so they dare to again.”

### Personality (core)
- Earnest to a fault; teenage certainty over caution
- Thin-skinned about “wrong” — embarrassment turns into fury, not jokes
- Loyal to unfinished things (and to Mira) harder than to rules
- Not charming-rogue; more clenched jaw, gold ribbon shaking in his hand
- Humor is dry / accidental misresolve, not quip machine
- **Privilege tell:** assumes time and materials are normal; shocked (later) that unplated people lose Forms forever as everyday weather
- **Shame tell:** when praised for Stack work he did “correctly,” he feels nothing; when someone likes his crooked companion, he almost can’t look at them

### Contradictions (where the character lives)
- Wants to be taken seriously by the academy **and** refuses to draw the way they grade
- Protects one life fiercely while being careless with process (sticky Marks, no stop-point)
- High-class coat + tarp project in the street like a secret shrine
- Loves Mira’s steadiness; experiences it as both safety and judgment
- After the wound: hates Freelining **and** feels dead when he isn’t near a Mark
- Will run into a Hard Draft for a Form, then run from people who need him

### How he moves / behaves (show, don’t speech)
- **With the book:** opens it like a door he’s not allowed to use; pages already dense with abandoned Marks; **gold fingers** (Freeliner tell) — stained cuticles/tips that never fully wash off; Act 1 he doesn’t hide them; exile he may try
- **With the tarp:** checks ropes, listens for packing sounds, talks under his breath to the shell (not full conversations — mutters, corrections, “stay”)
- **In class:** body present, eyes elsewhere; finishes drills early wrong or late perfect-and-empty
- **Under praise:** stiffens
- **Under correction:** goes hot fast — not witty, not loud at first; then too loud

### Inner life (what he won’t say)

### Skill portrait (reconcile: passionate ≠ academy genius)
He **is** passionate, driven, and capable enough to keep a living companion packing under a tarp for days. That is real Freeline strength. What he is *not*: the academy’s idea of good.

- **Academy “bad at drawing”:** Marks won’t Index clean. Crooked, sticky, overcommitted, wrong proportions, won’t stop when the rubric says stop. Teachers read that as unfocused / dangerous — not as empty talent.
- **Actually good at:** sustaining a Hold, listening to Potential in a shell, caring a Form into aliveness, stubborn iteration, reading a hungry Draft in his body. Smart — Potential-smart / making-smart — not lecture-smart.
- **Not a genius:** no effortless mastery, no secret top-rank reveal. He works hard and still Resolves wrong. The companion is proof of **devotion + Freeline instinct**, not prodigy flex.
- **Weak:** clean Indexable Marks, knowing when to stop, social camouflage, Stack drills
- **Strong:** will, tarp patience, living Forms, committing when others Index-and-quit
- **Wrongness (later virtue):** doesn’t match plates — catches Hard Drafts that eat On-Stack patterns
- **Training gap:** taught **Warden-track Freeline** — controlled, licensed, stop-when-ordered — not folk Freeline. He already “learns Freeline” at school; exile teaches him Freeline as everyone’s art, not a robe privilege. They don’t make him a genius; they change what Freeline *is for*, then he masters *his* line.
- **Mastery means:** ownership, control of sticky/plate-bleed risk, courage to Freeline in public again — **not** clean Indexable Marks. Wobble stays the signature.

**One line:** The academy grades *legibility*. Quill is skilled at *life*. Those are different tests.

### Exit beat — kicked out, shunned, why he leaves, why he goes far (lean lock)

**Hybrid still:** scene-flee from the disaster → short aftermath → institutional kill shot → city exit.

#### Where he lived (lean)
- **Boards at the academy** (dorm / house crest quarters). Privilege = the school *is* home most days.
- Family/house exists in the Luna city but is **status-tied**: crest, plates, Warden-respectable. After the disaster they **shun** him (door closed, name a stain) *or* are powerless under Safe-Only and choose the city over him. Either way: **no soft landing**.

#### What the ban scene says (must land)
- Expulsion speech = citizen lie: Freeline dangerous → lower cities in ruins → stamp; leave creating to Wardens/elders.
- Subtext the audience can feel (and Quill may not yet): Wardens Freeline; he Freelined *wrong / outside the robe*.
- His gold fingers held up as exhibit. Living unplated Hold + plate-bleed named as proof.
- Safe-Only birth: citizen Freeline crushed harder; academy track may narrow to “only sworn Wardens touch gold” — Quill barred from both street and robe.


#### Why he leaves the city (not “adventure”)
Not wanderlust. **Nowhere left inside:**
1. Academy housing gone
2. Family/house shuns or cannot keep him
3. Street is hostile — scapegoat; gold hands = target
4. Mira cannot safely hide him long (peer tether, not a fortress)
5. Vow + shame: he believes the doctrine for a while (“I am the ruin”)

He leaves because staying is arrest, violence, or a slow zero — not because he wants a quest.

#### Why he goes *far* / to the low cities
- Near-ring towns are Luna satellites: same stamps, same sermons, will turn him in.
- The **low cities** are exactly what they cursed in the expulsion speech — the place the lie points. Shame-logic: go where ruined people go / disappear among gold fingers / punish himself with the “proof.”
- Story-logic: only far enough from Luna presses does he see folk Freeline as normal and Potential-alive streets that aren’t High Luna tidy.
- He does not need a map prophecy; he needs **no safe nearby door**. Far is what exile is.

#### Mira
- Sees expulsion / gate leave if possible — second wound.
- Stays. Becomes the reason **to return** later (not the reason he stays).

#### Return pull + rage sequence (lean lock — emotional spine)

**Not:** he already knows they knew, then strolls home.  
**Yes:** hope first → door slam → betrayal → rage.

1. **Journey discovery (low cities / folk):** Making **feeds** Potential. Commonfolk read the field better than Luna — tapped in (hands, air, drafts, gold that sings). Quill learns the ecology from people Luna called ruined.
2. **Decision:** He has to **go tell the city**. Messenger motive. Earnest. Still a little academy in him — believes if they *know*, they’ll change. Mira can amplify (“come home and say it”) but the engine is **warn them**.
3. **Arrival:** They **won’t listen**. Stamp-only walls, Safe-Only law, his name is poison, Wardens wave him off, citizens fear gold fingers. Hard Draft may already be bruising the sky — still no ears.
4. **Rage turn (high energy):** He discovers they **KNEW** and **don’t CARE** — kings of ruins. Hope detonates into Yeager fury. This is a peak episode/climactic emotional bomb — not a quiet hallway chat.
5. **Action after rage:** He Freelines anyway (catch Hard Draft / crack the street open to making) — authorship as indictment, not a polite report.

Debt + failed messenger + Mira + rage > shame. Not an army. Not homesickness alone.
