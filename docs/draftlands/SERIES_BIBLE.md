## LOCKED (do not reopen without an explicit decision)

1. **Potential** — name of the ambient dust / field of becoming.
2. **Knowledge split** — everyone knows Potential exists early (like wind). Nobody starts knowing the ecology: making circulates it, stillness / purity-panic / Safe-Only laws starve it. That circulation rule is the season discovery for hero + audience. Do **not** treat Potential itself as a mid-season secret reveal.
3. **Otherwhere / uploads** — not villain cargo. 3rd-party / shared Forms count as making. “From-scratch purity” is antagonist ideology; the show argues remix + share + build-together.
4. **Resolve** — the *act* of packing a Mark into a Form. Never also the dust name.
5. **Toybox** — not a character / mascot; at most inferred (Outside / Oybo).
6. **Not everyone is a full-time Maker** — making is ambient culture, not universal job title. Wardens, traders, kids, people who mostly *use* Forms, etc.
6b. **Modalities are specialties, not castes** — technically anyone can Stack, Freeline, Craftbound, or pull Otherwhere. People have a **preference and specialty**; labels (Freeliner, Stacker, Indexer…) describe how you usually make, not what you’re allowed to touch. Gatekeeping after the disaster is politics, not physics.
7. **Post-disaster freeze is scoped** — after the hero’s disaster, home does **not** stop all making. **Freeliners** are banned, shamed, or driven underground. Stackers + Craftbound keep running. The city looks orderly and “safe” while Potential still thins — because the messy channel that circulates the field hardest was cut.
8. **Freeliners / Freelining** — freehand / slate / draw-speak-resolve specialty (was Gestureborn). Not `-born`. **Freelining is unpredictable** — that volatility is why people fear it and why Safe-Only bans it after the disaster. Not a caste issue; a reliability issue.
9. **Stack vocabulary** — catalog clean-cube culture. Formal: **Stackers** / **From the Stack**. Slang/roles: **Indexers**, **Cubers**, **Stampers** (the shell and/or the operator).
10. **Hero age lane** — mid-teen (~15–16), Eren Yeager altitude: intensity / guilt-rage / vow weight. Not a little kid.

## Core premise (working)

An endless, contrasting world of constant becoming — themed regions, chaos, things coming into existence. No one in-world knows where existence comes from (myth fragments only). **Potential** (the ambient medium of becoming) is thinning. That scarcity causes **worse drafts** in some places and **spreading deserts** in others. When societies outlaw or fear **messy making** (Freeliners) and retreat to only “safe” Stack patterns, the field starves further. The story argues — without speeches — that **we must keep building, including the dangerous kind.**

## World physics (felt, not explained)



### Drafts

- Massive **weather-scale** events of becoming
- Can be soft (gentler births) or **bad** (destructive creation: new things spawn by ripping through / overwriting what exists)
- Calendar, lore, drama, set pieces
- Never fully explained cosmologically



### Potential — LOCKED

- Always a little in the air (Dune-spice ecology + Star Wars Force feel)
- Thick in storms / blooms
- Absent in deserts / Quiet / Stillsky / Grainless reaches → creation famine
- Harvestable, political, sacred, contested
- **Living current (season truth):** using/making circulates it; stillness / hoarding / fear dries it
- Optional slang only: Drift, Firstgrain, Motes — never replace the locked name on the page



#### What people know vs what they learn — LOCKED

- **Known early (common knowledge):** Potential is real. You can see it, breathe it, feel when drafts are fat or thin. Deserts mean “no Potential.” Wardens work the weather. Kids know the word the way kids know “wind.”
- **Unknown / wrongly believed early:** *why* it’s thinning, and the loop that **making circulates it / not-making starves it**. Myths blame outsiders, Freeliner “wrongness,” Otherwhere arrivals, or “the world is ending so stop stirring it.”
- **Season discovery (hero + audience together):** the ecology. Stillness and purity-panic dry the field; shared making replenishes it. Potential is **not** a secret element reveal. The **rule of circulation** is the reveal.
- Audience may feel the print metaphor before characters do; characters never need a full cosmology.



### Wardens

- Can **harness / redirect / harden** drafts imperfectly — never fully control
- Levees and sails against a hurricane of becoming
- “For good” is contested (can spill Hard Drafts onto poorer districts)
- Naruto-adjacent readable technique; squad roles possible (Anchor / Cutter / Midwife / Spill)



### Coming into existence

- Mechanism **undetermined** cosmologically in-world; myths contradict
- **Visible birth (working):** most intentional Makes pass through a **Stamper** — see below
- Audience may eventually feel “print volume”; characters never need that word

### Stampers (working mechanics — visible Resolve)

The readable on-screen way a Mark becomes a Form. Not the cosmology. Not called a printer.

**What it looks like**

- A **shell of becoming** — often a **cube or rectilinear volume** for Stack / From the Stack work (clean edges, pale Potential fog inside, soft light).
- Freeliner Stampers can be **irregular** — sketch-shaped, lopsided, cracked, sketchscar-lined — same physics, messier silhouette.
- Otherwhere Stampers may arrive **already mid-fill** or with foreign markings (shared pattern, not blank slate).
- Outside the shell: air thickens with Potential; inside, the Form packs until the shell sheds / dissolves at Resolve.

**Time ≈ complexity (working)**

- **Small / simple** (bolt, cup, little companion) → near-instant or a breath. Kids can Stamper something in a scene without waiting.
- **Medium** → a scene beat / conversation / chase pause.
- **Large / complex** (vehicles, architecture, living multi-part Forms) → long holds; montage, night-to-morning, draft weather as clock.
- Thin Potential → Stampers **stall mid-shell**. Fat drafts → they finish too fast and spill / overwrite.

**By modality (feel)**

- **Stackers:** Index a known pattern → clean cube Stamper → predictable Resolve.
- **Craftbound:** tool/app-shaped Stampers (purpose-built).
- **Freeliners:** Mark first (draw / speak / slate) → Stamper grows to fit the Mark → Resolve. Wrong Marks → wrong shells.
- **Otherwhere:** shell may look “already started” — remix / finish counts as making.

**Story uses**

- Safe-Only streets still full of tidy Stack cubes; messy Freeline shells gone
- Hero’s vow: he can *watch* Stack Stampers; touching Freeliner shells is the relapse

**Language**

- **Stamper** = the shell (and slang for the operator). **Resolve** = the finish. Never “print” on screen.

## Modalities of making (Toybox stack → in-world cultures)

Do **not** call results “toys.” Prefer: Forms, Makes, Pieces, beings, creations.

> **LOCKED naming:** freehand = **Freeliners**. Catalog culture = **Stackers** / **From the Stack**, with slang **Indexers**, **Cubers**, **Stampers** (shell + operator). `-born` labels retired.

| Toybox real modality        | In-world working name | Feel |
| --------------------------- | --------------------- | ---- |
| Catalog → print             | **The Stack** / Stackers | Retrieve familiar patterns; clean cubes; status vs stagnation; **reliable** |
| Creator Space apps          | **Craftbound**        | Single-purpose guild crafts (Shapers, Softwrights, Gearbinders, Chromers…) |
| AI / finger-draw → fidelity | **Freeliners**        | Freehand Mark → Stamper → Resolve; messy, authorial, **unpredictable** — loved and feared for the same reason |
| Uploads from elsewhere      | **Otherwhere / Intake** | Forms that arrive already shaped (3rd-party / shared patterns); remix & build-with, not “less real” |

### Freeliners (explored deepest so far)

- Hero specialty. People who prefer / excel this way are called **Freeliners** — not a closed caste.
- **Core fear (LOCKED):** Freelining is **unpredictable**. Same Mark can Resolve crooked, overgrow, echo, or catch a draft wrong. Stack is boring because it’s *reliable*; Freeline is exciting because it isn’t.
- That’s why neighbors side-eye it, academies police it, and Safe-Only bans it after the hero’s disaster — “we can’t afford wild Stampers.”
- **Slate / device** can aim making via: draw, words (type/inscribe), speak, capture image/imprint
- Can also draw in open **Potential/drift** when thick
- Failures: misresolve, overgrow, echo, split, sticky sketch, draft bleed
- “Wrong” makes carry sketchscars; authorship visible



### Otherwhere / Intake (uploads) — LOCKED

- Real-world map: files / models from **elsewhere** (3rd-party sites, shared libraries) — not invented from a blank slate in front of you.
- Arrivals are **not** the villain. Bringing a Form in and building with it / for others **is making.**
- **Purity ideology (antagonist only):** “Only from-scratch is real. Printing / Resolving someone else’s Mark isn’t creation.”
- **Show thesis (behavior, not PSA):** using what others made, finishing it, combining it, shipping it to people — that *is* creation. Together you make **bigger** things than any solitary “pure” Mark.
- Drama: gatekeepers of purity / isolation after fear — not cursed cargo.
- Optional color: Otherwhere Forms often look “already finished” next to Freeliner sketchscars — difference, not ranking.



### Resolve (act)

- The moment a Mark packs into a finished Form
- Naming note: **Potential** = the field (locked). **Resolve** = the act of packing a Mark into a Form. Do not also name the dust Resolve.

## Who makes (LOCKED)

- **Not a planet of identical builders.** Most people live *among* Forms. Fewer are full-time Makers.
- **Anyone can use any modality.** Stack, Freeline, Craftbound, Otherwhere — the physics don’t care. What’s common is a **preference and specialty** (training, taste, local culture, tools on hand).
- Labels are soft: a “Stacker” is someone who *usually* pulls from the Stack; they might Freeline a birthday gift. A Freeliner might Index something urgent when freehand would take too long.
- **Stackers** and **Craftbound** feel ordinary, respectable, everywhere — the default economy of making.
- **Freeline** specialty is rarer as a serious path, flashier, **unpredictable**, easier to blame when drafts go wrong — academy kids, street sketchers, “unfocused” talents.
- **Otherwhere** sit beside them; purity panic often hits Freeliners and Otherwhere in the same breath.
- After the disaster, bans are **political** (“you may not Freeline here”), not “Freeliners are a different species.”
- Visual read when home goes bad: shelves still fill with Stack Forms; **messy Freeline shells vanish from the street**. Quiet creativity, not a dead city.

## Season 1 conflict (working root)

**Potential is becoming scarce.**

Symptoms (known early):

- Worse / more vicious drafts in some areas
- More deserts / Quiet spreading

Root (hidden early, revealed gradually):

- Field thinning / bottled / hoarded / unbalanced
- After the hero’s disaster, **his city bans or chokes Freeliner making** (fear + bad law + scapegoating him)
- Stackers/Craftbound continue — so the freeze is believable
- Cutting the messy channel **dries Potential further** → death spiral that looks like “we did the safe thing”

**Villain-shaped ideologies (menu):**

- Freezer (stillness = safety — rare extreme; usually loses to the softer lie below)
- Safe-Only / Anti-Freeliner (“Freelining is unpredictable; it caused the disaster; stay Stack”)
- Purifier / From-Scratchist (only “original” Marks count; Otherwhere and remixes are fake — **show does not side with them**)
- Steward who redirects “for the realm” onto the poor
- Harvester who stockpiles Potential
- Lie: “If we stop the dangerous kind of making, the world stabilizes.”  
- Truth: **hoarding + fear of messy / shared making starve the field; making together — including Freeliners and remix — circulates Potential.**

Analogy (never spoken as PSA): a creative economy that only allows catalog-safe output slowly dies; freestyle and sharing keep it alive.

## Protagonist (working)

- **Age (LOCKED lane):** mid-teen — roughly **15–16**. Think **Eren Yeager** (Attack on Titan): raw drive, guilt that turns into fury, old enough for the vow and the exile to land hard. Not elementary / “cute kid who messes up.”
- A **Freeliner** who is **not** strong (skill), but **is** intense (will)
- Draws “bad”; Resolves wrong; doesn’t apply lessons the academy way
- Public embarrassment; adults call him dangerous / unfocused — he hears it as a verdict, not a scolding
- Later read: his wrongness listens to scarcity / need / something the curriculum wasn’t built for
- **Not renamed yet**

### Wound / flight

- Cold open energy: *strong draft coming* → he runs **into** it (mystery motive — teenage certainty, not childish curiosity)
- He **causes a disaster** (misresolve / channel break / overwrite)
- Vows **never to build again** — Yeager-scale oath, not a sulk
- **Flees** (guilt + blame + self-hatred)



### Vow craft rule

- Keep the vow as emotional scar
- **Do not** honor full-season abstinence — kids need making on screen
- He breaks it: accidental → secret → necessary → public/climactic



### Arc

“My Freeline making destroys” → “Their ban on our making destroys deeper” → “I must make so they dare to again”

## Structure (working)

Not a beat-for-beat Lion King copy. Keep emotional rhyme:

**Belong → wound → flight → road of mystery → return → restore the cycle of making**

Chassis: season-scale **Hero’s Journey** + action-mystery serial.  
Opening: **action + mystery**, no explanation.  
Fighting: not the main dish — running, weather, pursuit, moral conflict; people-fights when systems push.

### Opening (desired)

- “They say a strong draft is coming”
- He heads into it
- Watcher wonders: What’s a draft? Why is the world changing? Why is he going in?
- Ends in disaster → flee



### Mid

- Exile road through changing Draftlands
- Avoids **his** making (Freeliner vow); world shows symptoms (worse drafts + deserts)
- Allies (outcasts, misresolves, underground Freeliners, maybe a truth-bringer from home)
- Clues toward scarcity / hoarding / Safe-Only laws



### Return home

- City not empty — **Stack busy, Freeline gone**
- Freeliners banned / shamed after his disaster; streets feel thinner in Potential even when shelves look full
- That “safety” helped dry the field



### Climax (goosebump target)

- Massive Hard Draft over home
- He returns and **builds epically** as a Freeliner — everything learned, especially his wrongness
- Crooked Mark catches/redirects the hungry draft, helps **release/restore Potential**
- City breathes; **people dare Freeline / share again** (second goosebump beat)
- Thesis without speech: a city that only allows safe making will rot; a city that risks messy making together can drink the sky again
- He is not suddenly perfect — wobble is the signature

## Themes (Toybox-rhymed)

- Messy ideas deserve to become real
- Fear of making starves everyone
- Building together beats hoarding “safety”
- Imperfect / wrong makes still matter
- Remixing and sharing count — you don’t have to invent alone to be a maker
- Together, shared Forms become bigger than any solitary “pure” Mark
- More making → healthier field → more making (creative economy as ecology)
- Play / care / naming stabilize Forms (inferred print-and-play urge)

Tone: **serious / earnest** (HP altitude) with room for misresolve comedy and friendship warmth. Protect appetite to build every episode.
