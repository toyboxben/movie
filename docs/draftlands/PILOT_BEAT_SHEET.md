## Audience teaching rule (LOCKED for pilot craft)

**Show the machine before anyone names it.**

Watchers should understand **stamping** from pictures in the first minutes:

1. Someone selects / opens a **known pattern** (plate page / clean book)
2. A **clean cube shell** appears (Stamper)
3. Potential fog packs a **familiar Form** inside
4. Shell sheds → Form exists → people treat it as normal

Only then may a character say “stamp” / “Stamper” in the expulsion speech — and the audience already has the picture.

Same for **Freeline**: Quill’s gold finger → crooked line on paper → ribbon peels off → **tarp** swells / irregular shell. No lecture.

**Draft:** sky/weather of becoming — shown before explained.

## A. Cold open — beat by beat (teach without wiki)

### A1 — Quiet High Luna street (30–60s of picture)

- Clean architecture, repeated Forms (same pet/helper face twice in frame = plates)
- Citizen **stamps**: clean cube blooms on a stoop, fog packs, shell sheds, ordinary life. No dialogue needed. Optional single line from a vendor/kid: “Stamp’s ready.”
- **Gold-finger contrast:** most hands clean / gloved. Quill’s hands already gold when we find him.

**Watcher knows:** here, making = tidy cube → known thing.

### A2 — Quill at the tarp (Belong in the hook)

- Academy coat, crest, book
- Tarp in the street — wrong silhouette under it; packing sounds; gold ribbon from his page into the tarp ropes
- He is mid-Hold on the **companion** (lopsided, one eye clearer — glimpse under tarp edge)
- Not Index-pretty. Passionate. Checking ropes like a nurse

**Watcher knows:** his making is different (messy, personal, slow).

### A3 — Warning

- “Strong Draft coming” (warden bell / sky bruise / people sealing)
- Citizens pull in stamps / cubes; doors close
- **Mira** finds him — grabs coat, begs him inside
- He looks at the tarp: *no plate / can’t leave it*

### A4 — He runs into the Draft

- Book open, gold ribbon, teenage certainty
- Mira’s face = tether wound #1

### A5 — Disaster

- Holding-shell (ribbon seals tarp)
- Sticky Mark → Draft treats it as plate → street presses redraw into *his* companion line
- Tarp rips; companion huge/wrong; district becoming *it*
- Scene-flee (shame/panic)

## B–D. Aftermath (character + doctrine)

### B1 — Immediate

### C1 — Expulsion (now “stamp” is safe to say)

Academy voice, on the nose (citizen lie):

- Freeline is dangerous  
- That’s why the **lower cities** are ruins  
- In High Luna we **stamp** and leave creating to **Wardens / elders**

Optional picture earlier in Belong: academy courtyard — students with gold fingers drilling Freeline under a master (so watchers know school Freeline exists). Quill’s tarp = Freeline **without leave**. Expulsion bars him from street *and* robe.

Crest pulled. Boarding gone. Family shun beat (door / crest). Safe-Only born with his name.

**Watcher feels:** the tidy cubes from A1 were ideology, not just tech.

### D1 — Exit
