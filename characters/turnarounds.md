# Character Turnarounds — Paste-Ready Generation Prompts

**Generate every sheet in this file before you make a single video clip.** Every shot in
the film inherits these. A bad turnaround at this stage is a bad film at shot 80.

---

## How to use this file

1. Generate in an **image** model with strong style control — Nano Banana Pro, Seedream, or
   Higgsfield's own image generation. Not a video model.
2. Generate each sheet **as a single multi-view image** where possible (the prompts below
   are written for that), *and* generate the individual views separately. You want both:
   the multi-view sheet is the best Elements reference, the individual views are the best
   keyframe starting points.
3. Approve, then freeze. Save to `refs/<character>/`. **Never regenerate a sheet
   mid-production.**
4. Register with the platform: **Kit → Soul ID.** Everyone else → **Elements**.

### Universal suffix — append to EVERY prompt in this file

```
STYLE: painterly 3D animation, hand-painted textures over dimensional forms, visible
brushstroke in the shadows, thick graphic silhouettes, painted volumetric light, rich
saturated color with deep teal shadows and warm amber key light, subtle film grain.
Character sheet on a flat neutral mid-grey background, even three-point studio lighting,
full body visible, consistent scale and consistent eye-line across all views, no cropping.
No text, no labels, no logos, no watermarks.
```

### Universal negative

```
NEGATIVE: photorealistic, live action, uncanny realistic skin, glossy plastic CGI sheen,
flat lighting, muddy color, extra limbs, extra fingers, deformed hands, warped face,
inconsistent proportions between views, text, labels, watermark, logo, anime, flat cel
shading, cluttered background
```

# 1. KIT — Soul ID set (36 images)

Kit is human, so he gets Higgsfield's **Soul ID** — trained on 20–80 photos of a person,
and by far the strongest identity lock available to us. Consistency of *face* across the
set matters more than variety of pose. Generate all 36, cull anything off-model, and train
on what survives.

## 1a. Turnaround sheet

```
Character turnaround sheet, six views in a single horizontal row, all at identical scale
and identical eye-line: front view, three-quarter left, full left profile, three-quarter
right, full right profile, and rear view.

KIT: an 11-year-old boy, mixed Black and East Asian, warm brown skin, round open face with
a scattering of freckles across the nose and cheeks, wide dark brown eyes, thick dark
eyebrows, a gap between his front teeth, and a permanent half-grin like he is about to
tell you something. Dense dark brown coils cropped short at the sides and taller on top,
slightly flattened on one side. He wears an oversized mustard-yellow hoodie with the
sleeves shoved up to the elbows, teal cargo shorts with bulging pockets, tall mismatched
socks, and battered red canvas high-tops laced with one red lace and one white lace. A
worn canvas satchel slung across his body. A stubby flat red carpenter's pencil tucked
behind his right ear. Kit's skin is smooth and painterly with completely smooth skin and
no striations of any kind.

Neutral relaxed expression, arms at his sides, standing straight. Identical clothing,
identical proportions and identical hair in every view.
```

## 1d. Camera-height studies (4)

```
Four views of the same boy, each from a dramatically different camera height, full body,
neutral grey background, identical lighting.

KIT: [paste the KIT PROMPT BLOCK]
```

> View 1 is the single most important reference image in the entire production. The camera

## 1e. Hand studies (4)

```
Four close-up studies of the same boy's hands, neutral grey background, even lighting.
Warm brown skin, an 11-year-old boy's hands, correct anatomy, exactly five fingers per
hand. 1: holding a stubby flat red carpenter's pencil in a writing grip. 2: mid-air
drawing gesture with the pencil, a glowing amber filament trail curling from the tip and
Ben-Day halftone dots blooming in the shadows around the hand. 3: open palm facing up.
4: reaching down toward the ground as if to pick up something very small.
```

## 1f. Lighting studies (6)

```
Six head-and-shoulders portraits of the same boy, identical neutral expression and
identical angle in all six, differing only in lighting.

KIT: [paste the KIT PROMPT BLOCK]

1: warm amber key light from camera left. 2: deep teal rim light from behind, dark front.
```

# 2. JUNE — Elements set

> **The trap:** her wireframe is on **her own left**, so it appears on **frame right** when
> she faces camera. Models flip this constantly, and they will also try to fill the
> wireframe in. It is empty — you can see the background straight through it. Cull
> aggressively.

## 2a. Turnaround sheet

```
Character turnaround sheet, six views in a single horizontal row, all at identical scale
and identical eye-line: front view, three-quarter left, full left profile, three-quarter
right, full right profile, and rear view.

JUNE: a girl-shaped printed creature about two feet tall, clearly hand-designed and
one-of-a-kind rather than mass-produced — an inventive original design with a rounded
angular head, large expressive dark eyes, a small simple mouth, and a swept crest of
sculpted material instead of hair. Her RIGHT side is fully complete: solid, beautifully
finished, printed in deep plum and burnt orange with crisp stacked horizontal 3D-printed
```

# 3. COLOSSUS — Elements set, three states

> **The trap:** RULE 11. He never has a back half. Models will complete him because dogs
> have four legs. Expect to cull most of your first batch, and generate the profile view
> more times than anything else — it is the angle that sells the missing half.

## 3a. Unfinished turnaround

```
Character turnaround sheet, five views in a single horizontal row at identical scale and
eye-line: front, three-quarter, full side profile, direct rear, and top-down.

COLOSSUS: a small printed robot dog, about eight inches tall, that is only forty percent
built. He has a boxy angular head with two round amber lens eyes and one folded ear, a
chest, and two sturdy front legs — and then his body simply STOPS in a clean flat vertical
cross-section just behind the shoulders, cut off mid-layer, hollow and open so the empty
interior lattice inside him is visible. He has NO back half, NO hind legs, NO hindquarters
and NO tail whatsoever — his body ends abruptly at the shoulders. He is printed in bright
cobalt blue with orange accent panels, with crisp stacked horizontal 3D-printed layer
striations across every surface.

The side profile view must clearly show that he is only a front half. The rear view looks
directly into the open hollow cross-section where the rest of his body should be.
```

## 3b. Cross-section detail

```
Extreme close-up of the cut-off rear cross-section of a small cobalt blue printed robot
dog — a clean flat vertical face where the body abruptly ends just behind the front
shoulders, showing the hollow interior with a visible honeycomb infill lattice inside, the
topmost printed layer left mid-ring and unfinished. Warm raking light picking out every
horizontal layer line.
```

## 3c. Pose sheet (5)

```
Character pose sheet, five full-body views of the same forty-percent-built robot dog, all
at identical scale. In every single view he has only a front half — no hind legs, no
hindquarters, no tail.

COLOSSUS: [paste the COLOSSUS PROMPT BLOCK]

Poses: 1 dragging himself forward enthusiastically on his two front legs with his cut-off
rear end trailing, 2 propped upright and sitting back on his open cross-section, 3 fallen
over on his side, 4 looking directly up with his head tilted and amber eyes bright,
5 mid-fall, front legs splayed.
```

## 3d. Smoothed state

```
Two views, front and side profile, at identical scale.

COLOSSUS-SMOOTHED: a small forty-percent-built robot dog, blank featureless matte white —
all colour drained away, all layer striations dissolved into a glassy seamless surface,
his amber lens eyes sealed over into smooth blank domes with no features. Frozen
completely motionless, caught mid-stride with one front paw raised off the ground. He
still has only a front half — no hind legs, no hindquarters, no tail. Cold flat lighting
with no rim light and no shadow.
```

## 3e. Finished state

```
Character turnaround sheet, four views in a row at identical scale: front,
three-quarter, full side profile, rear.

COLOSSUS-FINISHED: a small printed robot dog, now complete but visibly finished in a hurry
by hand. His front half is bright cobalt blue with orange accent panels and crisp
horizontal 3D-printed layer striations. His new back half is a mismatched warm amber,
noticeably a different scale from the front, with one hind leg longer than the other, an
over-built lumpy seam running across his middle where the new material meets the old, and
a tail that is little more than a thick scribbled loop of material. He stands crooked and
lopsided on four uneven legs.
```

# 5. THE TOWER — Elements set

> **The trap:** ten blocks, always, and the gradient runs crisp at the bottom to melted at
> the top. Never reversed. Count the blocks on every keyframe.

## 5a. Turnaround

```
Character turnaround sheet, four views in a single horizontal row at identical scale:
front, three-quarter, side profile, rear.

THE TOWER: a stack of exactly ten cube-shaped printed blocks balanced one on top of
another, about three feet tall, each block roughly the size of a fist. The stack shifts in
quality from bottom to top: the lowest three blocks are sharp-edged, matte, pale grey and
slightly under-extruded with visible gaps between the layer lines; the middle blocks are
clean and well-formed in warming amber tones; the upper blocks become progressively
glossier, sagging, over-extruded and drooping at the corners, trailing wispy strands of
stray material; the single topmost block is a shapeless melted blob of dark amber plastic.
Each block has one single horizontal slot across its front face like a mail slot. No arms,
no legs, no face.

Exactly ten blocks. Crisp at the bottom, melted at the top.
```

## 5b. Gradient details (3)

```
Three macro detail shots of a stack of printed plastic cubes: 1 the bottom three blocks,
sharp-edged, matte pale grey, slightly under-built with visible gaps between the
horizontal layer lines. 2 the middle blocks, clean and crisply formed in warm amber with
perfect even layer lines. 3 the top four blocks, glossy, sagging, over-built and drooping
at the corners with wispy stray strands trailing between them, topped by a shapeless
melted blob.
```

## 5c. Slot-glow states

```
Three identical front-on views of the same ten-block stack, differing only in which
horizontal front slots are lit from within with a warm glow: 1 all ten slots dark,
2 a single slot glowing near the bottom, 3 five different slots glowing at once at
different intensities. Identical framing and exterior lighting in all three.
```

## 5d. Poses (4)

```
Four views of the same ten-block stack at identical scale: 1 standing balanced upright,
2 mid-topple, leaning past the point of no return with blocks beginning to separate,
3 collapsed into a scattered pile of ten loose blocks on the ground, 4 the full stack
being carried awkwardly under someone's arm.
```

## 5e. Damaged state (SH-068 onward)

```
Front view of the same ten-block stack, but the bottom four blocks are now blank
featureless matte white with no layer lines, no texture and no slot, completely dead and
inert. The six surviving blocks above them retain their colour, their layer lines and
their glowing horizontal slots.
```

## 5f. Darkness state

```
The same ten-block stack in near-total darkness, lit only by the warm glow spilling from
its own horizontal front slots, which throw light onto the ground and up the sides of the
stack. Deep charcoal background, no other light source.
```

# 6. THE FINISHER — Elements set

> **The traps:** zero layer striations anywhere on his body, no rim light even when
> everything around him has one, and he never emotes physically — upright and calm in every
> frame. Models will texture him to match the world. Reject those.

## 6a. Turnaround

```
Character turnaround sheet, five views in a single horizontal row at identical scale and
eye-line: front, three-quarter left, full left profile, three-quarter right, rear.

THE FINISHER: a tall slender featureless humanoid figure moulded from perfectly seamless
matte-white material with absolutely no layer lines, no seams, no texture and no visible
joints anywhere on his body. Smooth blank oval head with no face at all — no eyes, no
mouth, no nose, only an unbroken curved surface with a faint pearlescent sheen. Long
elegant limbs, narrow shoulders, elongated smooth hands with fingers slightly too long. He
wears nothing. He stands perfectly upright with patient, unhurried composure, arms at his
sides.

His surface is completely smooth and completely untextured. He casts no visible shadow and
light falls on him flatly with no rim highlight.
```

## 6b. Head study

```
Four close-up views of the same featureless white head at identical scale — front,
three-quarter, full profile, rear — a smooth blank oval with absolutely no face, no eyes,
no mouth, no nose, no indentations and no features of any kind, only an unbroken curved
matte-white surface with a faint pearlescent sheen. Soft even lighting from four
directions confirming there is genuinely nothing there.
```

## 6c. Hands

```
Three close-up studies of the same tall smooth white figure's hands: elongated, seamless,
matte white, fingers slightly too long, no visible knuckles or joints or nails. 1 hanging
relaxed at his side. 2 one hand extended forward, palm up, in a gesture of offering.
3 one hand resting gently on top of a small brightly-coloured printed toy, where the toy's
horizontal layer lines are visibly beginning to dissolve into glassy blankness beneath his
palm.
```

## 6d. Context studies (3)

```
Three full-body views of the same tall featureless smooth white figure, identical pose,
in three different environments: 1 standing in a blown-out flat white void with no shadow
and no horizon, where he is almost invisible and read only by his faint edge. 2 standing
among small brightly-coloured printed toys in a warm honey-gold environment, where he is
violently out of place. 3 backlit standing in a bright doorway, in silhouette.

In every view he has no rim light, no cast shadow, and no surface texture whatsoever.
```

## 6e. Scale reference

```
Two views: 1 the tall featureless smooth white figure standing beside an 11-year-old boy,
where the figure is noticeably taller than the boy. 2 the same figure standing among small
printed toys roughly two feet tall, where he towers enormously over them. Identical
lighting, neutral grey background.
```

# 7. Background populations

Not characters — reference plates for crowd prompts, so that background continuity holds.

## 7a. The Brim (Raft crowd)

```
A dense crowd of small living printed plastic objects at wildly mismatched scales, each a
different single flat filament colour, all with visible horizontal 3D-printed layer
striations: keychains, bag clips, cable organisers, tiny planters, phone stands, small
figurines, hooks and brackets, all animate and in motion, jostling together in a market
crowd. Honey-gold late afternoon light.
```

## 7b. The Bin-Lost

```
A gathering of broken, warped and abandoned printed plastic objects in near darkness —
figures missing limbs, models with warped and curled-up bases, half-built shapes that stop
abruptly mid-layer, clumps of fused melted strands, things printed at the wrong scale.
They are not hostile or frightening, they are gentle and still and waiting. Near-monochrome
charcoal, a single pale shaft of light from far above.
```

## 7c. The Finished

```
A group of blank featureless matte-white figures of varying shapes and sizes, all
completely smooth with no layer lines, no colour and no features, standing utterly
motionless and frozen mid-gesture — one with an arm half raised, one mid-stride, one
turned as if it had been about to speak. Flat shadowless lighting. Absolute stillness.
```
