# COLOSSUS — The Companion

**Kit's robot dog. Forty percent printed. Eight inches tall. Named Colossus.**

---

## Who he is

Kit named him before he existed, which is the most Kit thing in the film. For a year the
robot dog he was going to build had a name and a legend and a whole personality, and then
the print started and stopped, and what actually exists is a front half.

He has a head, a chest, two front legs, and nothing behind that — the body just stops in a
clean cross-section, hollow, mid-ring. He drags himself around on his two front legs with
absolutely undiminished enthusiasm. He is fast. He is delighted. He falls over
constantly and does not care.

**Every character says his name with total sincerity.** Benchy says it. The Tower says it,
all ten of them. The Finisher says it with real tenderness. Nobody ever winks at the joke
and the film never acknowledges it, which is why it stays funny for ten minutes.

## What he's for

**He is Kit's shame, walking around in public.** That's the whole design.

Kit is embarrassed by him — because Colossus is the most visible possible evidence of what
Kit does to things, and he keeps following, and he keeps being happy about it. The
audience is ahead of Kit on this for the entire middle of the film, which is exactly where
you want them.

The setups, per the trope doc:

1. **SH-045** — Colossus catches up. Kit says "that's not mine." June hears.
2. **SH-050** — Colossus can't make a jump. Kit almost turns back and doesn't. June sees.
3. **SH-062** — Colossus brings him something useless and precious. Kit takes it without
   looking at him.
4. **Payoff, SH-071** — The Finisher takes him. Gently. As a mercy. Because a half-made
   thing is the most obvious suffering in the room, and he says so kindly, and he is not
   entirely wrong, and that's what makes it unbearable.

## The rebuild

In Seq 07 Kit finishes him with the pencil, in about six seconds, badly. The back half
that arrives does not match: wrong color, wrong scale, one hind leg longer than the other,
a tail that is essentially a scribble. He is lopsided and ridiculous and **whole**, and
the first thing he does is complete a bark for the first time in his life.

That bark is the emotional peak of the film. Do not spend a real bark before it.

## PROMPT BLOCK `COLOSSUS` (unfinished — SH-045 to SH-070) — paste verbatim

```
COLOSSUS: a small printed robot dog, about eight inches tall, that is only forty percent
built. He has a boxy angular head with two round amber lens eyes and one folded ear, a
chest, and two sturdy front legs — and then his body simply STOPS in a clean flat
cross-section just behind the shoulders, cut off mid-ring, hollow and open so you can see
straight into the empty interior lattice inside him. He has NO back half, NO hind legs and
NO tail whatsoever. He is printed in bright cobalt blue with orange accent panels, with
crisp stacked horizontal 3D-printed layer striations across every surface. He drags himself along enthusiastically
on his two front legs, hindquarters absent, moving fast and falling over often. His amber
lens eyes brighten when he is excited.
```

> **RULE 11 — Colossus never has a back half.** Not in a wide, not in shadow, not in a
> blurred background, not for a single frame. Models will complete him, because dogs have
> four legs. This is the highest-frequency continuity failure in the production. Check it
> on every keyframe he appears in, including ones where he is tiny in frame.

## PROMPT BLOCK `COLOSSUS_SMOOTHED` (SH-071 to SH-079) — paste verbatim

```
COLOSSUS-SMOOTHED: the small forty-percent-built robot dog, now blank featureless matte
white — all colour drained away, all layer striations dissolved into a glassy seamless
surface, his amber lens eyes sealed over into smooth blank domes. He is frozen completely
motionless, caught mid-stride with one front paw raised. He still has no back half. He
does not move at all.
```

**Do not animate him in these shots.** He is a statue. If the model gives him any motion
whatsoever, reject the clip.

## PROMPT BLOCK `COLOSSUS_FINISHED` (SH-080 onward) — paste verbatim

```
COLOSSUS-FINISHED: the small printed robot dog, now complete but visibly finished in a
hurry by hand. His front half is the original bright cobalt blue with orange accents and
crisp horizontal 3D-printed layer striations. His new back half is a mismatched warm amber, noticeably a
different scale from the front, with one hind leg longer than the other, an over-built
lumpy seam across his middle where new meets old, and a tail that is little more than a
thick scribbled loop of material. He stands crooked and lopsided on four uneven legs, tail
wagging hard.
```

## Reference set

All three states, separately. Paste-ready prompts in [turnarounds.md](turnarounds.md).

- Turnaround of the unfinished state: front, 3/4, **profile (critical — this is the angle
  that sells the missing half)**, rear (looking directly into the hollow cross-section),
  top-down
- Detail crop: the cut-off cross-section, showing the empty interior lattice
- Poses: dragging forward on the front legs, sitting propped up, fallen on his side,
  looking up, mid-fall
- Smoothed state: frozen mid-stride, one paw raised, front and profile
- Finished state: standing crooked, walking, the scribble tail
- Scale reference: beside Kit's hand, beside June, beside Benchy

## Voice

**Non-verbal.** No voice ID, no lipsync. His entire vocabulary is sound design, and it must
be built as a consistent sample library before production:

| Sound | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Two-tone rising boot-up chime | greeting, waking, delight |
| Rapid servo whirring | excitement, running |
| A bark that cuts off halfway | **his signature.** Used often. Never completes. |
| Descending three-note whine | disappointment, being left behind |
| Total silence | the smoothed shots. Nothing at all. |
| **A complete bark** | Used **once**, SH-080. Never before. |

The unfinished bark is a joke for eight minutes and then it is the saddest sound in the
film and then it is the best one. Protect it — do not let a full bark slip into any earlier
clip.
