## Job of the clip

**Action + mystery.** No jargon dump. Watcher should leave asking:

- What is thickening in the air?
- Why is everyone sealing up?
- Why is this boy running **into** it with a book?

Teach: Draft = weather of becoming (shown, not named). Quill = intense mid-teen Freeliner (gold fingers / gold line from book).

| Time | Picture |
|------|---------|

| 3–7s | Quill center frame — olive tunic, leather strap, thick bound book, bandage wrist, gold-stained fingertips. Looks **up** into the rising haze (match `var-v4-looking-up`). |
