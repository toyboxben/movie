# 03 — GEARHAVEN

**Seq 05. The midpoint. The archive, the truth, and the Finisher.**
**Category: robots, mechs, and functional prints.**

---

## What it is

An industrial city grown into the wall of the Bed, built from working printed mechanisms:
gears the size of buildings turning slowly against each other, printed chain drives,
hinges, brackets, clamps, threaded rods. Everything here moves, all the time, with a
purpose nobody can articulate.

It is inhabited by mechs — boxy, industrial, unhurried, running on visible printed gears
and pin joints. They are unionised. They are bureaucratic. They are deeply unhelpful, not
out of malice but out of procedure, and they are the funniest obstacle in the film because
they cannot be argued with, only filed against.

**The Archive** is at the centre: a cathedral of shelving holding the record of everything
that has ever been printed, one object per slot, going back to the beginning.

## Story function

- **The bureaucracy scene.** Kit's overselling meets an institution that is completely
  immune to charm. He has talked his way past everything until now, and here it does not
  work at all, which is the first real crack in him.
- **The Tower's finest hour.** Ten layers arguing simultaneously with a bureaucracy is the
  single best comedy set piece available to us. Give it room.
- **The half-answer.** The Hand did not break. It was **cut**. And the record shows it has
  stopped exactly once before — at the very beginning, on the very first print, which was a
  print of a person.
- **June's stake.** She learns the thing she has been waiting years for was murdered.
- **The Finisher arrives.** First meeting. He is warm, delighted, and entirely unhurried,
  and he knows about the drawer. He does not attack. He introduces himself and leaves.

## The beat that could only happen here

The archive is the only place in the world that keeps records, so it is the only place the
mystery can turn — and it's a bureaucracy, so the reveal has to be *requested in
triplicate*, which is the joke and the tension running in the same shot.

## Key light

**Camera right** — this is the one world where the key flips, deliberately, because it is
the turn. Cyan industrial glow from above, sodium-vapour orange practicals at ground level,
wet reflective floor. Every surface should be slick.

## The Hand from here

Visible through gaps in the machinery, **stopped and dark** in every shot in this
sequence. Use the stopped-state phrase from `worlds/README.md`. The characters keep
catching sight of it between the gears, and nobody comments after the first time.

## The Smooth horizon

Coverage rises from ~30 to ~45 across this sequence. Visible through the gaps in the
machinery on the wide shots. Values per shot in the CSV.

## Sound

Machine hum, hydraulic hiss, the deep slow grind of enormous gears, constant dripping,
sodium buzz. The Archive is different: the machine noise drops away almost completely and
is replaced by a huge dry acoustic and the sound of dust. It should feel like a library
inside an engine.
