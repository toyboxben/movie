# 05 — THE SMOOTH

**Seq 07. The climax.**
**Category: the absence of category.**

---

## What it is

Not a place. A **condition**, spreading. Where the Smooth has passed, the world is still
there — the hills are still hills, the buildings are still buildings — but everything has
been rendered blank flat white, without colour, texture, rings, shadow or edge definition.
It is legible as landscape only by the faintest gradient.

Standing in it are the Finished: everyone who used to live here, blank and motionless,
frozen mid-gesture, arranged exactly where they were when it reached them. A whole
population caught mid-sentence.

**This is the only world that breaks the lighting law.** No rim light. No shadow. No key
direction. Nothing separates a subject from the background, which after six sequences of
hard rim-lit graphic silhouettes feels physically wrong, and that wrongness is the entire
point. It should be uncomfortable to look at.

## Story function

- **The confrontation.** The Finisher explains himself. He is the first print. He came out
  wrong. He was thrown away. He is not lying and he is not exaggerating and the archive
  backed him up twenty seconds ago.
- **The offer.** He does not threaten Kit. He offers him exactly what Kit already wants:
  never finish anything, and nothing will ever be wrong. It is the most tempting thing
  anybody says in the film, and Kit hesitates, and the audience sees him hesitate.
- **The draw.** Kit finishes Colossus. Fast, ugly, no erasing, in about six seconds.
- **The crowd.** The Brim, watching from the edge, start drawing too. Badly. Hundreds of
  them. Hundreds of lopsided finished things arriving all at once.
- **He cannot smooth what is still being made.** Not a fight — an overwhelming. The colour
  comes back in a wave from the crowd inward.
- **The Hand restarts.**
- **June.** Kit finishes her in the four seconds he has left, while she is mid-sentence
  telling him not to.
- **He is yanked out.** The print completes and he goes, mid-word, without a goodbye.

## The beat that could only happen here

The visual argument of the climax is **colour returning to a white world**, and it is only
available in a location that has had all the colour taken out of it. Every earlier
sequence has been quietly banking saturation for this shot.

## Staging law

The Finisher never moves toward Kit. He stands still and talks. Kit does all the moving in
this sequence. If a shot has the Finisher advancing, it is wrong — the horror of him is
that he does not need to.

## Colour and the climax

The saturation curve across the film is a deliberate arc. Grade to it.

| Sequence | Saturation |
|---|---|
| 01 Bedroom | moderate, warm |
| 02–03 The Raft | **peak** — the warmest and richest the film ever gets |
| 04 Dino Basin | high but dusty |
| 05 Gearhaven | cool, moderate |
| 06 The Deep Bin | near zero — essentially monochrome |
| 07 The Smooth | **absolute zero** — pure white, no colour at all |
| 07 colour return | overshoot past the Raft's peak for roughly four seconds, then settle |
| 08 Bedroom | back to the opening, exactly |

The four-second overshoot at SH-081 is the most important grading decision in the film.
It's the emotional release, and it only works because the two sequences before it were
starved.

## Sound

Near-total silence with a thin high sine tone. **Subtractive, per `bible/04`:** ambience,
then reverb tails, then the low end have all been stripped by the time we arrive, so the
dialogue sits bone dry and too close.

When the colour returns, everything comes back at once — full ambience, full low end, the
crowd, and the stepper-motor rhythm of the Hand restarting, which is the first time the
film's signature sound has been heard since SH-043.
