# 01 — THE RAFT

**Seq 02 and 03. The wonder sequence, the mentor, and the crowning.**
**Category: everyday small prints — keychains, clips, organisers, planters, gadgets.**

---

## What it is

A harbour city built on the Bed — a vast warm glassy plain that stretches to the horizon,
textured like frosted glass, still faintly warm underfoot. The city grew outward from the
first thing ever made here, and it is constructed entirely from the discarded scaffolding
and rafts and brims of a million prints: a shanty-town of lattice towers, ramps, gantries
and walkways, lashed together, leaning, crowded, gorgeous.

Rivers of warm glowing material run through it in open channels. Everything is at the
wrong scale relative to everything else. A keychain the size of a bus is somebody's house.
A cathedral is a phone stand.

Overhead: the underside of a desk, impossibly far up, with cables descending through the
haze. And on the horizon, the Hand.

## Story function

**This is the Spirited Away sequence and it is protected.** Seq 02 is roughly
sixty-five seconds and is almost plotless on purpose — wide shots, held long, a boy
looking at a city. Do not put story in it.

Then Seq 03 turns the same location into a stage: the pen works, the crowd goes up, Kit is
crowned The Maker, June cuts him down in public, and the Hand stops.

## The rules established here

Through Kit breaking them, out loud, at volume — not through narration:

- Saying **"I made all of you"** produces total horrified silence. It's the worst sentence
  in the language, especially from somebody who just arrived through a Maker's line.
- The rivers are **potable, load-bearing, and sacred, in that order.** Kit drinks the
  river within four seconds of being told not to.
- Telling somebody their print looks **"clean"** is a grave insult.
- Kit has **no rings**, which is obscene, and everybody is being polite about it in a way
  he does not notice for a full minute.

## Wonder discipline — things at the edge of frame

Budget at least five unexplained things in this world. Never acknowledge any of them.

- A procession of identical small figures carrying one enormous key, chanting
- Someone selling cups of river by the ladleful, with a queue
- A festival happening two streets over that nobody mentions and we never reach
- Two large gears having an argument about something we join too late and leave too early
- A door standing freely in the middle of a plaza, with a queue of people waiting to go
  through it, opening onto nothing

## Key light

**Camera left**, honey-gold, low and late-afternoon. Long soft shadows running camera
right. This is the warmest the film ever gets and it should feel like the best afternoon
of your life, because everything after it is colder.

## The Hand from here

Visible on the horizon in every wide shot. See `worlds/README.md` for the three state
phrases. In Seq 02 it is building. At **SH-041** it stops, and the correct staging is:
everybody in the plaza looks up at once, and the sound drops out entirely.

## Sound

Creaking plastic, the lap and glug of the rivers, a constant crowd murmur, rigging
knocking, distant hammering, and under all of it the far-off stepper-motor rhythm of the
Hand. When the Hand stops, that rhythm stops, and the crowd noise stops half a second
later.
